By hand, with RCR, The timely delivery of radical radiotherapy, 4th ed. (2019), Appendix B, Eq. (B) on p. 26 and K = 0.9 Gy10/day, Tdelay = 28 days on p. 28: the planned course ends on day 25 < 28, so its tumour BED is 20 x 2.75 x 1.275 = 70.125 Gy10 with no repopulation term; the actual course ends on day 30, costing 2 x 0.9 = 1.8 Gy10; 7 x 3.50625 = 24.544 Gy10 was given before the gap, so 13 d (1 + d/10) = 70.125 - 24.544 + 1.8 = 47.381, d = 2.839 Gy. The most tempting wrong answer is 2.97 Gy: an onset of 21 days (or Eq. (B) applied below the onset) puts both courses past the onset, so the five extra days cost 4.5 Gy10.
