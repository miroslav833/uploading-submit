Key by hand: moire period L = 755.0 nm / 87 = 8.678 nm (twist 1.624 degrees). Dirac points of the two sheets k_theta = 4 pi/(3 L) = 4.827e8 1/m. hbar v k_theta = 0.2701 eV, alpha = w/(hbar v k_theta) = 0.4073. First-shell velocity v*/v = (1 - 3 alpha^2)/(1 + 6 alpha^2) = 0.2518, v* = 2.140e5 m/s. Eight cones (spin, valley, two moire Dirac points): n = 2 kF^2/pi, kF = 6.140e7 1/m. Cyclotron mass m* = hbar kF/v* = 3.026e-32 kg = 0.0332 me. Lifshitz-Kosevich factor X/sinh X = 0.1 at X = 4.500, X = 2 pi^2 kB T m*/(hbar e B), so T = 4.500 hbar e B/(2 pi^2 kB m*) = 2.30 K. Answer 2.3 K.

Most tempting wrong answer: a dot spacing judged by eye (about 7 px) gives about 109 periods and 3.9 K. Counting dots instead of periods (88) gives 2.4 K; four cones instead of eight give 1.6 K; the unrenormalised velocity gives 9.2 K.

Check program (standard library):
import math
hb, e, kB = 1.0546e-34, 1.602e-19, 1.381e-23
L = 755.0e-9 / 87
a = 0.1100 * e / (hb * 0.850e6 * 4 * math.pi / (3 * L))
vs = 0.850e6 * (1 - 3 * a * a) / (1 + 6 * a * a)
kF = math.sqrt(math.pi * 2.40e15 / 2)
m = hb * kF / vs
lo, hi = 1.0, 10.0
for _ in range(60):
    x = (lo + hi) / 2
    lo, hi = (x, hi) if x / math.sinh(x) > 0.1 else (lo, x)
print(x * hb * e * 0.250 / (2 * math.pi ** 2 * kB * m))   # 2.304
