(1): eps = V/(2.2 V + 0.3 sqrt(g D)), valid for holes above 0.2 cm (3.0 mm here). Limit eps = 1 - 1.2000/1.4530 = 0.17412. V = eps 0.3 sqrt(gD)/(1 - 2.2 eps) = 0.17412 x 0.37585/0.61693 = 0.10608 m/s. Q = 0.10608 x 0.020106 x 3600 = 7.678 m^3/h. The most tempting wrong answer uses the Akita-Yoshida correlation for air-water, eps/(1-eps)^4 = 0.2 Bo^(1/8) Ga^(1/12) Fr, which gives 8.0 m^3/h; the Hikita-Kikukawa power law 0.505 V^0.47 gives 7.5 and a drift-flux form with C0 = 1.2 gives 7.0.
