Key: 0.27 m (median stone size D50). In the attached notes it is the KEY line of the script (D50 = 0.2702 m). The deciding point is HEC-23, 3rd ed., Vol. 2 (FHWA-NHI-09-112, 2009), Design Guideline 14, p. DG14.8, item 2b: for SBR > 5 the overbank Q/A is compared with the velocity in the main channel within the bridge opening and the lower is used; the design example on p. DG14.13 (Step 3) takes the channel velocity as Qc/Ac, and the stem says the model gives only subsection averages. By hand: SBR = 40.0/3.40 = 11.8; overbank 201/79.0 = 2.544 m/s; channel 450/203 = 2.217 m/s, lower; Fr = 2.217/(9.81 x 1.45)^0.5 = 0.588 <= 0.80; D50 = 0.89 x 2.217^2/(9.81 x 1.65) = 0.270 m. The tempting answer is 0.36 m, from the overbank velocity without the check. Check program (standard library): V=min(201/79,450/203); print(0.89*V**2/(9.81*1.65))  prints 0.2702.
