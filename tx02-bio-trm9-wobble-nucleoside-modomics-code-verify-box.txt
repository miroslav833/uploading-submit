Key: 2000000253U. It stands in section 1 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 2. By hand: Trm9 with Trm112 makes the methyl ester of the wobble uridine side chain; with TRM9 deleted there is no 5-methoxycarbonylmethyl-2-thiouridine, and thiolation at C2 goes on. Table 1 of Chen et al., PLoS ONE 6, e20783 (2011), for tRNA-Glu(UUC) from the deletion strain: ncm5s2U 0.191, cm5s2U 0.013, mcm5s2U not detected (ratios to pseudouridine at 314 nm); wild type: mcm5s2U 0.220. The predominant thiolated nucleoside is 5-carbamoylmethyl-2-thiouridine (ncm5s2U), 15 times the acid. In the MODOMICS list of modifications (genesilico.pl/modomics/modifications) the row of 5-carbamoylmethyl-2-thiouridine, database ID 162, has 2000000253U under MODOMICS code new (2023). Most tempting wrong answer: 2000002540U, the code of 5-carboxymethyl-2-thiouridine (cm5s2U), reached by taking the substrate of the missing methyltransferase as the nucleoside that accumulates. The short form 253U is not what the 2023 column lists. Check in Python: rows={'cm5s2U':(0.013,'2000002540U'),'ncm5s2U':(0.191,'2000000253U')}; print(max(rows.values())[1]) prints 2000000253U.
