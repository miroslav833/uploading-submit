Data carried by the figure, measured on the stored image (300 x 300 pixels per frame, counts read back exactly from the grey levels): frame R mean 30.47, variance 54.49, B = 1.79; frame P mean 30.49, variance 213.9, B = 7.02; calibration frames read back at their labelled B. Key by hand: B_P - 1 = eps(1 + mu) for Poisson loading, so N = <k>/(B_P - B_R) = 30.5/5.23 = 5.83 particles in 0.60 fL, c = 5.83/(6.022e23 x 0.60e-15 L) = 16 nmol/L. Most tempting wrong answer: frames matched to the nearest calibration frames (P as 8.0, R as 1.5), 13 nmol/L; fixed loading B_P - 1 = eps mu gives 14 nmol/L.
