Data carried by the figure, measured on the stored image (600 x 600 pixels per frame, counts read back exactly from the grey levels): frame R mean 29.99, variance 53.81, B = 1.79; frame P mean 29.96, variance 209.7, B = 7.00; calibration frames read back at their labelled B. Key by hand: B_P - 1 = eps(1 + mu) for Poisson loading, so N = <k>/(B_P - B_R) = 30.0/5.21 = 5.76 particles in 0.60 fL, c = 5.76/(6.022e23 x 0.60e-15 L) = 16 nmol/L. Any B_P - B_R from 5.04 to 5.35 rounds to 16; 200 analyses (whole frame, central regions, tiles, single quadrants; 100, 70 and 50 percent image size; five calibration fits) give 15.70 to 16.45 nmol/L (notes section 3).
