(19)); the simple rule Ra/(L/d) <= 100 (Eq. (20)) holds only for L/d >= 20. Here L/d = 2.20: the bracket is 0.11744 + 0.02126 = 0.13870, so Ra_max = sqrt(0.1/(1e-6 x 0.13870)) = 849.1.
