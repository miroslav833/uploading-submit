Key: 550 kN (551.8 kN) largest transverse force. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY 40 % of nominal F_max = 551.8 kN" of key.py and "largest F with every group at or above 40 % of nominal: 551.7 kN" of independent.py. W = 18148.5 kN; nominal rear group load W x 9.90/44 = 4083.4 kN. DNV-OS-H201 (April 2012) Sec.3 D305 c), page 23: minimum calculated load on any trailer suspension system not less than 40% of its calculated nominal load (nominal: no horizontal loads, no CoG tolerance), so the limit is 1633.4 kN. Least loaded rear group with the CoG at x = 8.9 m, y = 0.400 m: W x 8.9/44 - (0.400 W + 9.00 F)/6.00 = 2461.0 - 1.500 F. F = (2461.0 - 1633.4)/1.500 = 551.8 kN. The most tempting wrong answer, 1600 kN, lets the group fall to zero (2461.0/1.500 = 1640.7 kN). Check: W=1850*9.81; print((W*8.9/44-0.4*W/6-0.4*W*9.9/44)*6/9) prints 551.8. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-04/OS-H201.pdf, page 23.
