Key: BGC0002392. It stands in the attached notes in the output of the first script (line KEY) and in the last line of the output of the second. By hand: a class I terpene synthase that turns geranylgeranyl diphosphate, without copalyl diphosphate, into a fourteen-membered ring fused to a gem-dimethylcyclopropane is a casbene synthase. In rice this is OsTPS28 in the chromosome 7 cluster DGC7, whose two CYP71Z enzymes give 5,10-diketo-casbene; the cluster was selected for in japonica (Zhan et al., Nat. Plants 6, 1447 (2020)). MIBiG 4.0 files it as BGC0002392, compound 10-oxodepressin, with this paper as reference. The most tempting wrong answers are BGC0000671, the momilactone cluster, and BGC0000672, the phytocassane and oryzalide cluster: both are labdane-related and begin with a class II copalyl diphosphate synthase, which the cluster of the question does not have. BGC0002388 is a retired second entry of the chromosome 2 cluster.
