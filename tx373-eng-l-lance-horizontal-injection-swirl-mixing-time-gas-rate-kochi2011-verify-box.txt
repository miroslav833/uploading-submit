Key: 19.6 L/min. It stands in the attached notes in the first section (output line 'KEY Q_g = 3.2708e-04 m3/s = 19.625 L/min') and in the second section (bisection, 19.625 L/min). By hand, with Kochi et al., ISIJ International 51 (3), 344-349 (2011), Eq. (15): T_m (H_L/D)(g/D)^(1/2) = 6.32e3 (H_L/D)^(5/2) Re^-0.47, Re = 4 Q_g/(pi D nu). H_L/D = 1.2000, so Re^0.47 = 6320 x 1.2^1.5 x (0.2/9.81)^0.5/31.00 = 38.265, Re = 2331.7 (range 588 to 4460), Q_g = Re pi D nu/4 = 2331.7 x pi x 0.2 x 0.893e-6/4 = 3.2708e-4 m3/s = 19.6 L/min. The most tempting wrong answer is 3.14 L/min, from the well-known bottom-blown equation T_m (H_L/D)(g/D)^(1/2) = 4.21e3 Re^-0.47 (Eq. (11) of the paper), equivalent in SI units to T_m = 1200 Q_g^-0.47 D^1.97 H_L^-1 nu^0.47, which does not apply to the swirling bath; a solver who knows only that equation lands there. Check program (Python, standard library): import math; Re=(6320*1.2**1.5*math.sqrt(0.2/9.81)/31.0)**(1/0.47); print(round(Re,1), round(Re*math.pi*0.2*0.893e-6/4*6e4,3)). It prints 2331.7 and 19.625. Source: the paper named above, Eqs. (12), (13) and (15), pp. 347-348, https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/isijinternational/51/3/51_3_344/_article.
