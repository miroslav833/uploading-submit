Key: 0.308 cm^3/s (0.3083 before rounding). In the attached notes it is the line 'KEY' of the first script and the first crossing in the second. Kurabayasi 1961 (Bulletin of the JSME 4, 539-546), Eq. (1), p. 540: d[um] = 8.10e3 (rho/mu)^0.25 sqrt(D_n) (v_n sqrt(D_r))^0.4 / v; rho g/cm3, mu mP, D_n mm, D_r cm, v_n and v = sqrt(v_n^2 + v_p^2) in m/s. Here mu = 17.90 mP, K = 8.10e3 x 0.49561 x 0.63246 x 1.58489 = 4024.0, v_p = 12.5664 m/s. d(v_n) = K v_n^0.4/sqrt(v_n^2 + v_p^2) peaks at v_n = sqrt(2/3) v_p = 10.26 m/s (629.5 um), so 450 um has two roots: v_n = 2.4531 m/s (q = 0.3083 cm3/s) and 34.78 m/s (4.371 cm3/s, beyond the 1.00 cm3/s pump limit). Most tempting wrong answer: 0.294 cm3/s with the peripheral velocity alone in the denominator. Check program (Python, standard library): import math; K=8.10e3*(1.080/17.90)**0.25*0.4**0.5*10**0.2; vp=math.pi*0.1*40; a,b=0.01,10.0; exec('m=(a+b)/2\nif K*m**0.4/math.hypot(m,vp)<450: a=m\nelse: b=m\n'*60); print(round(a*math.pi*0.4e-3**2/4*1e6,4)) prints 0.3083.
