Each wire lies midway between two bright lines and there is one bright line per wavelength, so the 1876 mm, a perpendicular distance, hold 141 wavelengths.

Key by hand. Wavelength 1876 mm/141 = 13.305 mm, a = 2 pi d/lambda = 14.167. Rotating layer, stress-free surfaces, steady onset: R(a) = ((pi^2 + a^2)^3 + pi^2 T)/a^2 with T = 4 Omega^2 d^4/nu^2; the minimum over a lies where 2 a^6 + 3 pi^2 a^4 = pi^6 + pi^2 T. So T = (2 a^6 + 3 pi^2 a^4 - pi^6)/pi^2 = (1.6172e7 + 1.193e6 - 961)/9.8696 = 1.7593e6, sqrt(T) = 1326.4, and Omega = nu sqrt(T)/(2 d^2) = 5.578e-4 x 1326.4 = 0.740 rad/s (7.1 rev/min), so 0.74. The rate changes by about 2 per cent per line (140 lines 0.725, 142 lines 0.755). The centrifugal acceleration 1 m from the axis is 6 per cent of gravity.

Other values. Rapid-rotation limit a^6 = pi^2 T/2 alone: 0.71. A bright line taken for each roll (wavelength of two spacings): 0.10. Factor 4 dropped in the Taylor number: 1.5. Answer left in revolutions per second: 0.12. A count of 120 lines: 0.46.
