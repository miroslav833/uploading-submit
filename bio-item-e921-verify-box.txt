By hand, a pure myopathy with mitochondrial DNA at 14 % of controls is a depletion syndrome. In his fibroblast mitochondria thymidine and deoxycytidine phosphorylation are 12 % and 16 % of control and the purines are normal; TK2 phosphorylates the pyrimidine deoxynucleosides and DGUOK the purines, so TK2 is deficient. Onset at 5 years, after walking at 13 months, is childhood onset (over 1 and under 12 years, Garone et al. 2018). Orphanet (Orphadata June 2026) lists the TK2 myopathic form as ORPHA:254875 with three onset entries below it, and the childhood-onset one is ORPHA:730743, which has no narrower entry. The most tempting wrong answer is 254875, the TK2 myopathic form, which is right but not the most detailed entry; reading the assay as a purine kinase defect gives 279934, the DGUOK hepatocerebral form.
