The answer is not in a paper. It follows from the number of inner electrodes, which only the micrograph shows, and from the Landau free energy given in the question. The uploaded notes contain the scripts and their output.

What the micrograph shows. Key by hand. Entropy per unit volume s = s0(T) - P^2/(2 eps0 C), so the ceramic under field gives off T P^2/(2 eps0 C) and the chip warms by f T P^2/(2 eps0 C c), f = 0.522. Repeat distance 0.4960 mm/116 = 4.2759 um, less 1.10 um of nickel: d = 3.1759 um, E = 8.00 V/d = 2.5190 MV/m. alpha = 32.0/(8.8542e-12 x 1.200e5) = 3.0118e7 V m/C. alpha P + beta P^3 = E gives P = 0.06998 C/m^2 at 320.0 K and 0.06980 C/m^2 at the final temperature. Rise = 0.522 x 320.13 x 0.06980^2/(2.12501e-6 x 2.95e6) = 0.1299 K. Answer 130 mK. Counts from 115 to 119 electrodes give 125.4 to 134.5 mK, the same two digits.

Most tempting wrong answers. The stack height is also 124 x 4.0 um; taking 124 layers (125 electrodes) from that round number in place of the count gives 148.6 mK, that is 150 mK. An estimate from a judged spacing of about 5 px over the whole stack, in place of a count, gives 125 to 130 electrodes and 149 to 161 mK. A linear dielectric (P = E/alpha) gives 184 mK; the voltage divided by the full repeat distance gives 81 mK.

Check program (Python 3, standard library; prints 3.1759 2.519 0.0698 129.9):

eps0, C, T0, beta, c = 8.8541878e-12, 1.200e5, 288.0, 1.200e9, 2.95e6
N, T, V, f = 117, 320.0, 8.00, 0.522
d = 0.4960e-3 / (N - 1) - 1.10e-6
E, x = V / d, 0.0
for _ in range(50):
    a = (T + x - T0) / (eps0 * C)
    P = E / a
    for _ in range(50):
        P -= (a * P + beta * P ** 3 - E) / (a + 3 * beta * P * P)
    x = f * (T + x) * P * P / (2 * eps0 * C * c)
print(round(d * 1e6, 4), round(E / 1e6, 4), round(P, 5), round(1e3 * x, 1))
