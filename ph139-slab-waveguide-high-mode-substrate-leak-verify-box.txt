The answer is not in a paper. It follows from the mode order, which only the figure gives, and from the theory of planar waveguides (citations above). The uploaded notes contain the scripts and their output.

What the figure shows. 57 bright horizontal bands, evenly spaced by 5.3 px. Their centres on the middle of the frame, in px from the top: 23.9 29.1 34.4 39.8 45.1 50.3 55.7 61.0 66.2 71.5 76.9 82.1 87.4 92.8 98.0 103.3 108.7 114.0 119.2 124.5 129.8 135.1 140.4 145.8 151.0 156.3 161.7 166.9 172.2 177.6 182.9 188.1 193.4 198.8 204.0 209.3 214.7 220.0 225.2 230.6 235.9 241.1 246.4 251.8 257.1 262.3 267.7 273.0 278.2 283.6 288.9 294.1 299.4 304.8 310.1 315.3 320.9. A TE mode of order m has m + 1 intensity maxima, so m = 56.

Key by hand. k = 2 pi/0.8500 um = 7.39198 /um. Transverse resonance kx d = 56 pi + arctan(gc/kx) + arctan(gb/kx) gives kx = 4.03287 /um, beta = 10.88217 /um, N = 1.472158, gc = 7.9862 /um, gb = 1.77242 /um, ks = 24.6888 /um in the silicon. Loss per reflection at the oxide T = 4 sin(2 Phi_b) sin(2 psi) exp(-2 gb t) = 4 x 0.73669 x 0.14284 x 1.4464e-2 = 6.088e-3, with Phi_b = arctan(gb/kx) and psi = arctan(gb/ks). Effective thickness 44.00 + 1/gc + 1/gb = 44.689 um, 41.46 reflections per cm, alpha = 0.2524 per cm = 1.096 dB/cm. The complex root of the four-layer round-trip condition gives 1.120 dB/cm and a finite-difference calculation 1.119 dB/cm. Answer 1.1 dB/cm.

Most tempting wrong answer. Setting the mode order equal to the number of bands (m = 57) gives 1.4 dB/cm. One band fewer or more in the count gives 0.88 or 1.4 dB/cm, the highest guided mode (m = 61) gives 4.5 dB/cm, and T = exp(-2 gb t) without the prefactor gives 2.6 dB/cm.

Check program (Python 3, standard library, prints N = 1.472158, T = 6.0883e-03 and an attenuation of 1.0963 dB/cm):

import math
lam, nf, nb, ns, d, t, m = 0.8500, 1.5700, 1.4525, 3.650, 44.00, 1.195, 56
k = 2 * math.pi / lam
f = lambda kx: (kx * d - m * math.pi - math.atan2(math.sqrt(k * k * (nf * nf - 1) - kx * kx), kx)
                - math.atan2(math.sqrt(max(k * k * (nf * nf - nb * nb) - kx * kx, 0)), kx))
lo, hi = 1e-9, k * math.sqrt(nf * nf - nb * nb)
for _ in range(200):
    mid = (lo + hi) / 2
    lo, hi = (lo, mid) if f(mid) > 0 else (mid, hi)
kx = lo
b = math.sqrt(k * k * nf * nf - kx * kx)
gc, gb, ks = math.sqrt(b * b - k * k), math.sqrt(b * b - k * k * nb * nb), math.sqrt(k * k * ns * ns - b * b)
T = 4 * math.sin(2 * math.atan2(gb, kx)) * math.sin(2 * math.atan2(gb, ks)) * math.exp(-2 * gb * t)
print(b / k, T, 10 / math.log(10) * 1e4 * T * kx / (2 * b * (d + 1 / gc + 1 / gb)))
