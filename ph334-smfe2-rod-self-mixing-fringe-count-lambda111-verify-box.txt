Key by hand. Abrupt falls mean a receding face, so the rod shortens. |dL| = 139 x 785.0 nm/2 = 54.557 um; lambda_s = -54.557 um/64.10 mm = -851.1 ppm (strain from the ideal demagnetized state to saturation along the field). Orientation average for grains strained alike: lambda_s = (2 lambda_100 + 3 lambda_111)/5, so lambda_111 = (5 x (-851.1) - 2 x 60.0)/3 = -1458.6 ppm, -1460 ppm.

Other values. Sign read the other way: +1380. One fringe per wavelength: -2880. Measured strain taken as (3/2) lambda_s: -986. Weights swapped: -2220. 138 or 140 fringes: -1450 or -1470.

ls = -139*785.0e-9/2/64.10e-3
