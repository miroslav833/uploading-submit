The key is a band count. Between base and T there are nine broad light bands, so nine years in 84 mm, 9.33 mm per year. The two thin bright lines at 30.1 and 67.3 mm, each just above a summer band, are the stress bands of the two bleachings and are not years. Counted as years they give 11 and 7.6 mm per year.

Band layout from the drawing program (mm from base, low-density band then high-density band):
1: 0.0-5.3, 5.3-9.6
2: 9.6-14.8, 14.8-19.0
3: 19.0-24.6, 24.6-29.1
4: 29.1-33.5, 33.5-37.1 (stress line 30.1)
5: 37.1-42.4, 42.4-46.8
6: 46.8-52.2, 52.2-56.7
7: 56.7-62.0, 62.0-66.3
8: 66.3-70.8, 70.8-74.5 (stress line 67.3)
9: 74.5-79.7, 79.7-84.0 (T)

Python check:
years = [9.6, 9.4, 10.1, 8.0, 9.7, 9.9, 9.6, 8.2, 9.5]
