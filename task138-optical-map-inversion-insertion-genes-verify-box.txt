Key by hand. The contig runs opposite to the reference: r = 746.0 - s matches 55 of 61 labels within 1 kb (the same orientation matches 23 at best). Two labels (134.0 and 556.0 kb) and two reference sites (300.5 and 573.2 kb) are single differences. Four labels close together (264.7, 272.5, 291.9, 295.3 kb) and four reference sites in the same stretch (448.5, 456.5, 475.9, 479.2 kb) have no partner: each label misses a site by 2 to 3 kb (+2.1, -2.4, -2.4, +2.2 kb), the other 55 by at most 0.6 kb. Their spacings are 7.8, 19.4, 3.4 kb on the contig and 8.0, 19.4, 3.3 kb on the reference, both read left to right, so this segment is inverted and there r = s + 183.9. Copy 1: 746.0 - 57.0 = 689.0, 690. Copy 3: 746.0 - 497.0 = 249.0, 250. Copy 2 lies 2.3 kb right of the label at 264.7 kb, which is the site at 448.5 kb: 450.8, 450.

The most tempting wrong answer is 690, 480, 250, from placing the whole contig as one piece (746.0 - 267.0 = 479.0).

Check with Python (standard library): the four stray labels against the four unlabelled sites, as a rigid piece and inverted.
L = [264.7, 272.5, 291.9, 295.3]
R = [448.5, 456.5, 475.9, 479.2]
print([round(min((746.0 - s - r for r in R), key=abs), 1) for s in L])
d = sum(r - s for s, r in zip(L, R)) / 4
print(round(d, 1), [round(s + d - r, 1) for s, r in zip(L, R)], round(267.0 + d, 1))
