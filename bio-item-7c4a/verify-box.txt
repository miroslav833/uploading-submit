By hand: the hormone is fibroblast growth factor 23 (UniProtKB:Q9GZV9, 251 residues, signal peptide 1 to 24, mature chain 25 to 251). Residues 176 to 180 read RHTRS, so the reported cut between Arg-179 and Ser-180 follows the Arg-x-x-Arg convertase motif. GalNAc-T3, whose loss of function causes hyperphosphataemic familial tumoral calcinosis, glycosylates the one threonine inside that motif, Thr-178, and blocks the cut; its lectin domain makes that transfer depend on an earlier GalNAc at Thr-171. A molecule the convertase does not cut carries O-linked GalNAc (MOD:00164) at both Thr-171 and Thr-178, has lost the signal peptide and carries no phosphate, which is the term above.

Most tempting wrong answer: PR:000061154, the mature chain glycosylated at Thr-178 alone, which Reactome displays as GalNAc-T178-FGF23. A reader who stops at the sugar that blocks the convertase takes it, but its listed modifications leave out the Thr-171 sugar the protected species carries.
