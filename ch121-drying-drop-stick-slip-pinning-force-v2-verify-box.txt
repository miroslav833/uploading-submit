Key depends on the ring count: 18 rings, so 17 jumps. Not 19 or 21.

Centre pixel (1014, 991). Bar 300 px = 0.5 mm.
Ring crossings right of the centre, x in px:
1914 1861 1787 1736 1695 1647 1599 1562 1526 1484 1449 1422 1379 1353 1331 1304 1281 1256
Radii: 900 847 773 722 681 633 585 548 512 470 435 408 365 339 317 290 267 242. Closest two are 22 px apart.
Central spot, radius 80 px. That's the last water drying, not a pinned position: landing there would need (80/242)^3 = 0.04 of theta_0.

Thin cap V = (pi/4) theta R^3, jump at constant V, lands at theta_0. Per jump theta_r/theta_0 = (R_next/R)^3.
Product over 17 jumps: (242/900)^(3/17) = 0.793, theta_r = 7.14 deg.
f = 72.0 (cos 7.14 - cos 9.0) = 72.0 (0.99225 - 0.98769) = 0.329, so 0.33 mN/m.

Likely wrong: an extra ring or three read in, 0.31 (19 rings) or 0.29 (21). Spot counted as a ring, 0.49. One ring missed, 0.34.
