Key: 0.350 m. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3, where the measuring point is found by scanning the gunwale. By hand: 46 CFR 178.330(e) says the limits of heel are measured at the point of minimum freeboard, or at three-quarters of the vessel's length from the bow if the point of minimum freeboard is aft of this point. The minimum (0.40 m) is at the transom, aft of 0.75 x 14.0 = 10.5 m, so the limit is measured at 10.5 m, where the freeboard is 1.10 - 0.70 x 1.5/5.0 = 0.890 m. Paragraph (d)(3)(i): i = f(2L - 1.5L')/(4L) = 0.890 x 22.0/56.0 = 0.3496 m. Heel arctan(0.3496/2.10) = 9.5 degrees, under the 14 degrees of (d)(7). The most tempting wrong answer is 0.157 m, from the minimum freeboard at the transom (0.40 x 0.39286). Check in Python: L, Lc = 14.0, 4.0; x = 0.75*L; f = 1.10 + (0.40 - 1.10)*(x - 9.0)/(14.0 - 9.0); print(round(f*(2*L - 1.5*Lc)/(4*L), 4), round(0.40*(2*L - 1.5*Lc)/(4*L), 4)) prints 0.3496 0.1571.
