Key rests on the FIGO 2021 reference level for vulvar depth of invasion: basement membrane of the deepest adjacent dysplastic tumour-free rete ridge (Olawaiye et al. 2021, Table 3 note a). Not the papilla tips (2009 method), not the normal epidermis.

Read from the pixels of the figure (1200 x 555 px, bar 75 px = 0.5 mm, 150 px per mm):
dermal papilla tips in the HSIL: y 112
bases of the tumour-free dysplastic ridges either side of the focus (x 507 and 693): y 260
base of the ridge the carcinoma comes from (x 608): y 303 with the nests attached
short pegs of the mature epidermis at both edges: y 158
deepest invasive tumour: y 367 (x 607)

Key: (367 - 260)/150 = 0.71, so 0.7 mm. IA with 1.5 cm.
Likely wrong answer: 2009 method from the papilla tips, (367 - 112)/150 = 1.70 mm, IB.
Also wrong: from the normal pegs, (367 - 158)/150 = 1.39 mm.

Check, standard-library Python
ppm = 150
