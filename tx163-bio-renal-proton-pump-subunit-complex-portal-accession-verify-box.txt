Key: CPX-6912. The key is in the first section of the attached notes (output line KEY) and again in the second section (line code).
By hand. Normal anion gap acidosis, low potassium, urine pH above 6.5 with bicarbonate 12 mmol/L and nephrocalcinosis give distal renal tubular acidosis. The large membrane subunit with the two proton half-channels, beside the proteolipid ring, is subunit a of the V0 sector of the vacuolar ATPase. Of the four genes for subunit a, ATP6V0A4 is the one of the type A intercalated cells (Smith et al. 2000). Open www.ebi.ac.uk/complexportal and search ATP6V0A4. One curated human entry comes back, Vacuolar proton translocating ATPase complex, ATP6V0A4 variant, CPX-6912. The rest are predicted entries with numbers above CPX-10000.
Wrong answers that tempt most. CPX-6905 is the osteoclast pump with a3, and CPX-6904 is the Golgi pump with a2. A solver who knows these two and counts on gives CPX-6906, which is an immunoglobulin entry. CPX-6913 is a guess one past the key and does not exist. CPX-2470 is the a1 pump.
Check in seconds (Python, standard library, needs the network):
import json, urllib.request
u = 'https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/complex-ws/search/ATP6V0A4?number=50'
d = json.load(urllib.request.urlopen(u))
print([e['complexAC'] for e in d['elements'] if not e['predictedComplex']])   # ['CPX-6912']
Citations. Smith et al., Nat Genet 26, 71 (2000). Wang et al., Mol Cell 80, 501 (2020). Meldal et al., Nucleic Acids Res 50, D578 (2022).
