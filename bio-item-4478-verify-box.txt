Key: 5.5 mm. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3 (the paper's tabulated lengths). By hand: Johnson et al., Am. J. Bot. 113, e70221 (2026), Methods: the functional (extended) proboscis length of Philoliche is the clypeus-to-tip length plus the length of the prementum, because in feeding the flies extend the proboscis by up to that length (Morita 2011); Table 1 gives 17.7 mm resting and 24.7 mm extended for P. gulosa; Table 2 gives a spur length of 30.2 mm at Kareedouw. 17.7 + 7.0 = 24.7 mm; 30.2 - 24.7 = 5.5 mm. The most tempting wrong answer is 12.5 mm, with the pinned length taken as the reach (30.2 - 17.7). Check in Python: print(round(30.2-(17.7+7.0),1)) prints 5.5. Citation: Johnson, Moir, Newman and Van der Niet, Am. J. Bot. 113, e70221 (2026), Methods and Tables 1 and 2.
