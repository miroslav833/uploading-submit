126 lines separate N = 127 ribbon layers.

By hand. Ribbon thickness t = 0.720 x 2.775 mm / 127 = 15.73 um. In a wide sheet with the alternating field H0 along it on both faces, d2H/dz2 = (i omega mu0 mur / rho) H, so H = H0 cosh(kz)/cosh(kt/2) with k = (1+i)/delta and delta = sqrt(2 rho/(omega mu0 mur)) = sqrt(1.200e-6/(pi x 1.000e5 x 4 pi e-7 x 3.000e4)) = 10.07 um. The mean field gives the complex permeability mu tanh(u)/u, u = (1+i) x/2, x = t/delta = 1.563. Reactance over resistance of a loss-free, leakage-free winding is mu'/mu'' = (sinh x + sin x)/(sinh x - sin x) = (2.2815 + 1.0000)/(2.2815 - 1.0000) = 2.56.

The most tempting wrong answer is 2.46, from the thin-sheet formula 6/x^2 = 6/1.5629^2. Forgetting the metal share (t = 2.775 mm/127 = 21.85 um) gives 1.47. 126 layers give 2.52 and 128 give 2.60.

Check (Python, standard library):
import math
t = 0.720*2.775e-3/127
d = math.sqrt(1.200e-6/(math.pi*100.0e3*4e-7*math.pi*3.000e4))
x = t/d
print(round((math.sinh(x) + math.sin(x))/(math.sinh(x) - math.sin(x)), 3))    # 2.56
