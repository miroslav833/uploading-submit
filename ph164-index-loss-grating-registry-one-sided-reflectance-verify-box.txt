The figure carries three things: the width of a tooth, the width of a stripe and the displacement of the stripes against the teeth. It is a simulated micrograph of 684 x 123 px with 38 periods of 18.0 px (a pixel with the index i reaches from i to i + 1): bright stripes in rows 21 to 44, the plain rib in rows 52 to 79, the teeth below it in rows 82 to 102. Edges in px from the left side of the picture, first six teeth: 2.1-11.1, 20.1-29.1, 38.1-47.1, 56.1-65.1, 74.1-83.2, 92.1-101.1; first six stripes: 2.0-6.8, 20.0-24.8, 38.0-42.8, 56.0-60.8, 74.0-78.7, 92.0-96.8. A tooth is 9.0 px wide, 4/8 of a period. A stripe is 4.5 px wide as drawn (4.8 px at half height after the blur), 2/8 of a period. The left edges coincide, so the stripe centres (4.4, 22.4, 40.4 px) lie 2.2 px, one eighth of a period, to the left of the tooth centres (6.6, 24.6, 42.6 px), the same over the whole row. The light travels to the right, so the stripes are displaced towards the entrance.

Key by hand. Complex index nbar + sum n_m exp(i m K x), x along the light, origin at a tooth centre, time factor exp(-i omega t). n_0 = i fs ns; n_(+-1) = (dn/pi) sin(pi ft) + i (ns/pi) sin(pi fs) exp(-+ i theta), theta = -pi/4. Coupled waves: A' = -sigma A + i k_plus B, B' = sigma B - i k_minus A, k = (2 pi/lambda) n, sigma = a - i delta. At 1536.50 nm: c_i = 2 x 4.00e-3/lambda = 5.207 /mm, c_l = 2 x 3.60e-3 x 0.7071/lambda = 3.313 /mm, a = 2 pi x 0.25 x 3.60e-3/lambda = 3.680 /mm, delta = 2 pi x 2.4000 x (1/1536.50 nm - 1/1536.00 nm) = -3.195 /mm. k_minus = c_i + i c_l exp(i theta) = 7.550 + 2.343 i, k_plus = 2.864 + 2.343 i (per mm). sigma = 3.680 + 3.195 i, gamma^2 = sigma^2 + k_plus k_minus = 19.47 + 47.91 i, gamma = 5.966 + 4.016 i, gamma + sigma = 9.646 + 7.210 i. Long grating: R = |k_minus|^2/|gamma + sigma|^2 = 62.49/145.0 = 0.431, that is 0.43.

The most tempting wrong answer is 0.094: the coupling constants exchanged or the displacement read to the right, 13.69/145.0 = 0.094, which is the reflectance from the other end. Stripes read as centred on the right-hand edges of the teeth, a quarter period to the right, give 0.034. Other values: displacement of a quarter period to the left 0.68; three eighths to the left 0.83; stripes centred on the teeth 0.24; over the gaps 0.53; one eighth and three eighths to the right 0.094 and 0.18; one coupling constant for both directions 0.24; loss grating left out of the coupling 0.23; no detuning 0.66; stripes taken as half a period wide and a quarter period to the left 0.37.

Check in a second (Python 3, standard library):
import cmath, math
nbar, dn, ns, lamB, lam, ft, fs, k = 2.4, 4.0e-3, 3.6e-3, 1536.0e-9, 1536.5e-9, 4/8, 2/8, -1
a = 2*math.pi*fs*ns/lam; d = 2*math.pi*nbar*(1/lam - 1/lamB)
ci = 2*dn*math.sin(math.pi*ft)/lam; cl = 2*ns*math.sin(math.pi*fs)/lam; th = 2*math.pi*k/8
km = ci + 1j*cl*cmath.exp(1j*th); kp = ci + 1j*cl*cmath.exp(-1j*th); s = a - 1j*d
g = cmath.sqrt(s*s + kp*km); g = g if g.real > 0 else -g
print(abs(km/(g + s))**2, abs(kp/(g + s))**2)      # 0.4308 the key, 0.0944 from the other end
