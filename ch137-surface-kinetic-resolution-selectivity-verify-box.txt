Working. The site term cancels in r_A/r_B, so ln(c_A/c_A0) = s ln(c_B/c_B0). Starting amounts 21 and 19 from the image.
s = ln(5/21)/ln(13/19) = 1.4351/0.3795 = 3.78.
Most tempting wrong answer: racemic start (or the Kagan formula, which assumes one): ln(5/20)/ln(13/20) = 3.22. Zero-order reading, converted ratio 16/6 = 2.67. One unconverted molecule given the wrong hand: 4.51 (start then looks racemic) or 3.20.
