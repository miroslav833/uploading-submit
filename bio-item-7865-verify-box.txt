Key: LMFA02020003. It stands in the attached notes in the output of the first script (line KEY) and in the last line of the output of the second. By hand: (9S,13S)-12-oxo-phytodienoic acid has both side chains on one face of the ring; OPR3 and the three rounds of beta-oxidation leave both ring centres as they are, so the C12 acid is the cis acid. With the shortened chain the descriptor at the old C-9 changes from S to R without any change in space, which gives (3R,7S), or (1R,2S) in ring numbering: (+)-7-iso-jasmonic acid, whose isoleucine conjugate is the hormone (Fonseca et al., Nat. Chem. Biol. 5, 344 (2009)). The LIPID MAPS Structure Database lists (1R,2S)-3-oxo-2-(2'Z-pentenyl)-cyclopentaneacetic acid under LMFA02020003. The most tempting wrong answer is LMFA02020001, the entry named Jasmonic acid: it is the (1R,2R) trans acid, the epimer at C-7 into which the first product goes over. LMFA02020017 is (1S,2S), what one gets by copying the two S descriptors of the precursor, and LMFA02020004 is the mirror image of the key. Check without the notes: in Python, import json, urllib.request; print(json.load(urllib.request.urlopen('https://www.lipidmaps.org/rest/compound/lm_id/LMFA02020003/all'))['sys_name']) prints (1R,2S)-3-oxo-2-(2'Z-pentenyl)-cyclopentaneacetic acid.
