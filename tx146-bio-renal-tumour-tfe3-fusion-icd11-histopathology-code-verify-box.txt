Key: XH1442. It stands in the attached notes in the output of the first script (line KEY) and as the entry left over in the output of the second. By hand: the tumour is a primary of the kidney in a child, with nuclear PAX8, diffuse nuclear TFE3, an ASPSCR1::TFE3 fusion and a balanced t(X;17)(p11.2;q25). This is the renal carcinoma with the fusion of alveolar soft part sarcoma described by Argani et al., Am. J. Pathol. 159, 179 (2001): a TFE3-rearranged (Xp11 translocation) renal cell carcinoma, one of the MiT family translocation carcinomas. In ICD-11 MMS, release 2026-01, Chapter X, Histopathology, block of malignant adenocarcinomas, the entry MiT Family translocation carcinomas has the code XH1442. The most tempting wrong answer is XH8V95, alveolar soft part sarcoma, reached by taking the fusion for the tumour's name; the sarcoma is PAX8 negative, arises in soft tissue and carries the unbalanced der(17)t(X;17) (Ladanyi et al., Oncogene 20, 48 (2001)). XH05V6 is renal cell carcinoma, NOS, and XH1D07 papillary renal cell carcinoma. The entry can be checked in the ICD-11 browser of WHO (icd.who.int, release 2026-01, Extension Codes, Histopathology, Adenomas and adenocarcinomas, Adenocarcinomas, malignant).
