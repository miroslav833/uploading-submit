Key: 47.7 m (47.69 m) least water depth over the bank. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY least water depth = 47.69 m" of key.py and "parabola on L = 650 m: depth 47.689 m" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H202 (October 2015) [4.2.7.4], page 34: "Ltowline = 1800 BP/FTD, but minimum 650 metres." Here 1800 x 36/108 = 600 m, so 650 m governs; sag 78.5 x 650^2/(8 x 110 000) = 37.69 m; + 10 m = 47.69 m. The most tempting wrong answer, 42.1 m, uses 600 m. Check: print(round(78.5*650**2/(8*110000)+10,2)) prints 47.69. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2015-10/OS-H202.pdf, page 34.
