Key: MONDO:0971005. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY, the child of MONDO:0008855 whose gene is CIITA) and again in section 3, where every other child is excluded by mechanism. By hand, absent HLA-DR, -DQ and -DP with normal class I, CD4 lymphopenia, hypogammaglobulinaemia and chronic cryptosporidiosis in an infant of first cousins are MHC class II deficiency. Loss of RFX5, RFXAP or RFXANK removes the RFX complex, so no X-box complex forms and the promoter is bare in vivo; loss of CIITA leaves RFX bound and the X and Y boxes occupied but silent. His gel shift is normal and his promoter is occupied with no HLA-DRA mRNA, and fusion with RFX5-deficient cells restores HLA-DR, so he has the CIITA form (group A). In Mondo (release 2026-11-03) that is MHC class II deficiency 1, MONDO:0971005, a child of MONDO:0008855 with no child of its own. The most tempting wrong answer is MONDO:0008855, the disease as a whole, which fits but is not the lowest term; reading the data as a bare promoter gives an RFX term such as MONDO:0971014.
