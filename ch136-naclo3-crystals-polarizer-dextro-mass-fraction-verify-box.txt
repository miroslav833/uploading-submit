Key depends on the hand of every crystal, read from its grey value against its edge.

Grey = 10470 sin^2(3.13 a + 0.31 deg) if dextrorotatory, 10470 sin^2(3.13 a - 0.31 deg) if levorotatory (a = edge in mm). Dextro is brighter at equal edge (analyser turned counterclockwise, dextro turns the plane clockwise).
