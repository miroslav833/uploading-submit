Key: MONDO:0958192. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY, the gene-defined child of MONDO:0011612 whose gene is AMT) and again in section 3, where each gene-defined child is scored against the assay profile. By hand, neonatal encephalopathy with plasma glycine 910 µmol/L, CSF to plasma ratio 0.14 and no ketosis or lactic acidosis is glycine encephalopathy. P-protein (GLDC) at 96 % and H-protein (GCSH) normal with T-protein (AMT) below 2 % locate the defect in AMT. In Mondo (release 2026-11-03) that is glycine encephalopathy 2, MONDO:0958192; the onset terms neonatal (0017353) and infantile (0017354) are not gene-defined. The most tempting wrong answer is MONDO:0958179, glycine encephalopathy 1, from assuming the commoner GLDC. Check in Python from the assay: obs={'P':96,'H':100,'T':2}; enc={'MONDO:0958179':'P','MONDO:0957382':'H','MONDO:0958192':'T'}; print(min(enc,key=lambda c:sum((obs[k]-(0 if k==enc[c] else 100))**2 for k in obs))) prints MONDO:0958192.
