(5) of Kajiuchi, Shiragami and Miyagawa, Kagaku Kogaku Ronbunshu 11 (1), 123 (1985): dp/(rho Ui^2) = 0.0264/(alpha Re^0.25) {(Di/Do)^3 - 1} + 0.529 {(Di/Do)^4 - 1}. Ui = 4 x 3.933e-4/(pi x 0.02500^2) = 0.8013 m/s, Re = 19992, rho Ui^2 = 640.78 Pa, Di/Do = 1.34409. Terms: 0.0264 x 1.4282/(0.0100 x 11.8909) = 0.3171 and 0.529 x 2.2637 = 1.1975; sum 1.5146; dp = 970.5 Pa. The most tempting wrong answer integrates the Blasius friction with the local Reynolds number, 0.0791/(3.75 x 0.0100 x 11.8909) x (1.34409^3.75 - 1) = 0.3603, which with the same kinetic-energy term gives 998.2 Pa, that is 1000 Pa. The paper scales its laminar term by the friction ratio at the inlet Reynolds number, so its exponent is 3 and its constant 0.0791/3 = 0.0264.
