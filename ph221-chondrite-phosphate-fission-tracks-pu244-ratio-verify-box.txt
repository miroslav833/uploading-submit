Total 186 / 5.480e-5 cm^2 = 3.39416e6 per cm^2; minus 3.00e5 cosmic and 4.8446e5 uranium leaves 2.60970e6 per cm^2 from 244Pu, so 244Pu at the start = 2.60970e6 / (6.375e-4 x 1.23e-3) = 3.3282e12 per cm^3. 238U at the start = 1.40138e15 x 1.99433 = 2.79481e15 per cm^3. The most tempting wrong answer is 2.4e-3, the ratio to today's 238U. Full length L instead of L/2 gives 4.8e-4; eta left out 9.8e-4; cosmic-ray or uranium tracks not removed 1.3e-3 or 1.4e-3.

A check in Python, standard library only:

import math
NA = 6.02214076e23
N8 = 180e-9*3.10*NA/238.05*0.99275
e = math.exp(1.55125e-10*4.45e9)
g = 0.850*15.0e-4/2
rU = g*N8*8.46e-17/1.55125e-10*(e - 1)
rPu = 186/5480e-8 - 3.00e5 - rU
print(rPu/(g*1.23e-3)/(N8*e))   # 1.191e-3
