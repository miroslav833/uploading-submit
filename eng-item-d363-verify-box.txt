Key: 1140 kN (1138 kN) largest sling force. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY SKL = 1.60, largest sling force = 1138.4 kN" of key.py and "4/5 of the hook load on one diagonal: 1138.4 kN per sling; 1.6 x basic: 1138.4 kN" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H205 (April 2014) Table 3-2, page 19, wire rope slings in a statically indeterminate four-point lift: 1.25 (diagonals within 0.3 %, 60 deg, DF <= 4.0), 1.33 (0.3 %, 70 deg, DF <= 5.0), 1.5 (0.5 %, 60 deg, DF <= 5.2), 1.6 (0.5 %, 70 deg, DF <= 5.6), E <= 70 000 MPa. Here 0.50 % and 66 deg leave only 1.6, and DF = 3800/711.5 = 5.34 is within 5.6. Basic sling force 2600/(4 sin 66.0 deg) = 711.5 kN; 1.6 x 711.5 = 1138 kN. The most tempting wrong answer, 889 kN, uses 1.25. Check: import math; print(round(1.6*2600/4/math.sin(math.radians(66)),1)) prints 1138.4. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-04/OS-H205.pdf, page 19.
