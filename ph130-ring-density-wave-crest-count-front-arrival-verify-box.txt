The figure (507 x 118 px) is a simulated strip of a ring image with two red marks above it, and one integer is read from it. The mark A stands over pixel column 40 and the mark B over column 470, 430 px apart, and each stands on a bright stripe, a crest of the wave. Between the two marked crests lie 62 dark stripes and 61 bright stripes, so B is 62 wavelengths from A, N = 62. The crest spacing shrinks from 9.5 px next to A to 5.4 px next to B, and the crest number grows as X^2 - X_A^2 with X the distance from the resonance, to within 0.02 of a wavelength.

Key by hand: the period ratio 14.400/9.600 = 3/2 = m/(m - 1) makes L the m = 3 inner Lindblad resonance. A tightly wound wave in a cold self-gravitating disc has (omega - m Omega)^2 = kappa^2 - 2 pi G sigma |k|, so k = D/(2 pi G sigma) with D = kappa^2 - m^2 (Omega - Omega_p)^2 = 3(m - 1) Omega_L^2 X/r_L near L, where X = r - r_L. The phase between A and B is 6 Omega_L^2 (X_B^2 - X_A^2)/(4 pi G sigma r_L) = 2 pi N. With Omega_L = 2 pi/9.600 h = 0.65450 per hour and X_B^2 - X_A^2 = 1687.75 km^2 this gives pi G sigma = 2.3512e-5 km/h^2 (sigma = 8.6 kg/m^2). A change of amplitude travels at the group velocity pi G sigma/kappa = 3.5924e-5 km/h = 0.315 km/yr, so the 21.50 km from A to B take 5.985e5 h = 68.27 yr, that is 68 yr. In closed form the delay is 4N/(3(m - 1)) x r_L/(X_A + X_B) x T_L = 41.333 x 1508.28 x 9.600 h; sigma and G drop out.

The most tempting wrong answers are 67 yr and 69 yr, from 61 or 63 wavelengths. Other wrong routes: 3 m Omega_L^2 in place of 3(m - 1) Omega_L^2 gives 46 yr, a group velocity of 2 pi G sigma/kappa gives 34 yr, the distance from A to B divided by N taken as the wavelength at B gives 54 yr and taken as the wavelength at A gives 94 yr, the phase counted from L instead of from A gives 46 yr, and the travel time from L to B is 160 yr.

Check in a second (Python 3, standard library). It prints the delay in years for 62, 61 and 63 wavelengths:
N, m, TL, rL, XA, XB = 62, 3, 9.600, 118400.0, 28.50, 50.00
t = lambda N: 4*N/(3*(m - 1))*rL/(XA + XB)*TL/8766
print(round(t(62), 2), round(t(61), 2), round(t(63), 2))   # 68.27 67.17 69.37
