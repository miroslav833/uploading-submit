Key: EC 5.6.2.7. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and as the last line of section 3. By hand: 3.4 and 2.9 per min with a 3' and a 5' tail and 0.4 per min on the blunt duplex show unwinding without polarity; the drop to 0.3 per min at 19 bp and below 0.005 per min at 25 bp shows that only short duplexes come apart; 1.1 per min with ADP-BeFx shows that strands separate on nucleotide binding without hydrolysis, so the enzyme does not translocate (Yang and Jankowsky 2006; Yang et al. 2007; Liu, Putnam and Jankowsky 2008). The Enzyme List gives EC 3.6.4.13 the history 'created 2009, deleted 2024' with the note that it is now covered by EC 5.6.2.5, 5.6.2.6 and 5.6.2.7, and describes EC 5.6.2.7, DEAD-box RNA helicase, systematic name RNA helicase (non-translocating), by exactly these properties. Most tempting wrong answer: EC 3.6.4.13, deleted in 2024. EC 5.6.2.6 (RNA 3'-5' helicase) and EC 5.6.2.5 (RNA 5'-3' helicase) are for translocating enzymes that need a tail of one polarity.
