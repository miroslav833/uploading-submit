Key: 1.5 MN (maximum vertical wave load on the span). In the attached notes it is the KEY line of key.py (1506.6 kN). The deciding point: diaphragms between the girders close the bays, so the uplift head is measured to the bottom of the diaphragms. HEC-25, 3rd ed. (FHWA-HIF-19-059, 2020), Section 11.4.1, p. 223, defines dz_v as the crest elevation minus 'the elevation of the underside of the bridge deck or the low-chord of the diaphragms under the deck if diaphragms are present'; the worked example on p. 225 uses the diaphragm bottom. By hand: crest = 2.46 + 1.3 x 1.40 = 4.28; dz_v = 4.28 - 3.50 = 0.78 m; Fv = 2 x 10.06 x 0.78 x 96.0 = 1507 kN. Tempting answers: 0.64 MN (slab underside) and 2.1 MN (girder bottom). Check program (standard library): print(round(2*10.06*(2.46+1.3*1.40-3.50)*12*8, 1))  prints 1506.6.
