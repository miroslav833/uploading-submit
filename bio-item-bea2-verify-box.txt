Key: RHEA:81479. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY, after the search of all 19,005 Rhea reactions) and again in section 3, where the base is derived by atom bookkeeping and the entry chosen by skeleton and mass change. By hand, 13-methyltetradecanoyl-CoA (iso-C15) plus L-serine minus CO2 gives a C17 3-oxo base, reduced to 15-methylhexadecasphinganine, C17H37NO2 (287.282 Da); the base found in the lipids, C17H35NO2 (285.267 Da), is 2.016 Da lighter, so the cytochrome b5 enzyme acting on the N-acylated base is the Delta4-desaturase, not a hydroxylase (which would add 15.995 Da). Rhea writes this as an N-acyl-15-methylhexadecasphinganine + 2 Fe(II)-[cytochrome b5] + O2 + 2 H(+) = an N-acyl-15-methylhexadecasphing-4-enine + 2 Fe(III)-[cytochrome b5] + 2 H2O, master RHEA:81479 (directed forms 81480 to 81482). The most tempting wrong answer is RHEA:46544, the same desaturation for the straight-chain C18 base, or a number guessed next to it such as RHEA:46548. RHEA:81475 is the 4-hydroxylation of the same branched substrate, and RHEA:34687 is an obsolete earlier entry.
