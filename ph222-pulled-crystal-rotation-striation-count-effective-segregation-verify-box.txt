Key by hand. Growth per revolution 34.10 mm/131 = 260.3 um; at 0.2000 rev/s the growth speed is v = 5.206e-5 m/s (3.12 mm/min). Rotating-disc layer: delta = 1.6 D^(1/3) nu^(1/6) omega^(-1/2) with omega = 1.2566 rad/s, D^(1/3) = 2.1544e-3, nu^(1/6) = 0.07162, omega^(-1/2) = 0.8921, so delta = 220.2 um. Steady layer: D C'' + v C' = 0, C = C_L at delta, v (1 - k0) C(0) = -D C'(0) at the face, which gives C_s/C_L = k0/(k0 + (1 - k0) exp(-v delta/D)). v delta/D = 1.147, exp(-1.147) = 0.3177, C_s/C_L = 0.0870/0.3771 = 0.2307, so 0.23. With the factor 1.61 in delta the value is 0.232. Counts from 129 to 134 all give 0.23.

Other values. The most tempting wrong answer is 0.087, the equilibrium coefficient with the build-up at the face left out. omega in revolutions per second: 0.63. Finite nu/D correction kept in delta (factor 1.151): 0.26. The form k0 exp(v delta/D): 0.27. The viscous layer sqrt(nu/omega) used for delta: 0.34. A count of 100 striations: 0.30.
