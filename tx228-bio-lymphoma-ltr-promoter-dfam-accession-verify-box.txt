Key: DF000000818, the Dfam curated family THE1B, a long terminal repeat of the MaLR group with a consensus of 364 bp. In the attached notes it is the KEY line of both scripts. By hand: the receptor of macrophage colony-stimulating factor is CSF1R. In Hodgkin lymphoma cells its transcripts begin in a derepressed long terminal repeat upstream of the myeloid promoter, and the primary paper names that repeat as THE1B of the MaLR family. The genome agrees: in assembly hg38 the one THE1B element of the locus lies at chr5:150,092,453-150,092,809 on the strand of the gene, and the reference transcript NM_001349736.2 has its first exon inside it, 6,068 bp upstream of the myeloid start. Dfam lists THE1B as DF000000818. The tempting wrong answers are the records next to it: DF000000819, the sister subfamily THE1C, and DF000000817, THE1-int, the internal region of the retrovirus. Check (Python, standard library): fetch https://dfam.org/api/families?name=THE1B with the header Accept: application/json and print the accession of the result whose name is exactly THE1B; it prints DF000000818. Lamprecht et al., Nat. Med. 16, 571 (2010); Storer et al., Mob. DNA 12, 2 (2021).
