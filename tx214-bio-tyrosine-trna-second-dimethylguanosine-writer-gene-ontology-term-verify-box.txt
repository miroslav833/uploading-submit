Key: GO:0160248. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and as the last line of section 3. By hand: X is position 26 and Y is position 27 of tRNA. Without A the mark at 26 is lost everywhere and the mark at 27 stays; without B the mark at 26 stays everywhere and the mark at 27 is lost; in both cases tRNA-Tyr keeps 1.0 dimethylguanosine and shows no monomethylguanosine. So the product of B adds both methyl groups to G27 and does not act at 26 (it is TRMT1L; Zhang et al. 2025; Hwang et al. 2025). In go-basic.obo with data-version releases/2026-07-26 the term defined as 'guanosine(27) in tRNA + 2 S-adenosyl-L-methionine = 2 H+ + N(2)-dimethylguanosine(27) in tRNA + 2 S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine' is tRNA (guanine(27)-N2)-dimethyltransferase activity, GO:0160248; it has no child terms. Most tempting wrong answer: GO:0160104, the term for position 26 alone, which is the activity of A (TRMT1). GO:0160103 covers positions 26 and 27 together (Aquifex aeolicus Trm1) and is excluded because the mark at 26 is lost when A alone is missing. GO:0004809 is obsolete. Check in Python (positions that keep the mark without A must equal the positions of the term): terms={'GO:0160104':{26},'GO:0160103':{26,27},'GO:0160248':{27}}; kept_without_A={27}; print([g for g,p in terms.items() if p==kept_without_A]) prints ['GO:0160248'].
