The figure is 1100 x 860 px, with no scale. Platinum strap to row 70, faint well line near row 170, bottom mirror from row 238 to row 786 at x = 550, GaN substrate below. AlInN is dark. Dark bands (AlInN layers) in the mirror: 63, 8.69 px per pair on average. Centre rows at x = 550 of every tenth band from the cavity down: 1: 244, 10: 322, 20: 409, 30: 496, 40: 583, 50: 670, 60: 758, 63: 783. Each pair is one AlInN and one GaN layer, and the GaN of the top pair merges into the cavity, so 63 pairs.

By hand. Bottom mirror between GaN cavity and GaN substrate: b = (2.2820/2.4750)^(2 x 63) = 3.6082e-5, T_b = 4b/(1+b)^2 = 1.4432e-4. Top mirror, 10 pairs SiO2/Ta2O5, air behind: Y = (1.4650/2.1500)^20 = 4.6557e-4, T_t = 4 x 2.4750 Y/(2.4750 + Y)^2 = 7.5215e-4. Round-trip loss T_b + T_t + 0.00440 = 5.2965e-3. Share through the substrate 1.4432e-4/5.2965e-3 = 0.02725, i.e. 0.027. 62 pairs give 0.032 and 64 give 0.023; the rounding of the givens keeps 0.0271 to 0.0274.

The tempting slips: no cavity loss 0.16; top transmittance 4Y without 1/n0 0.023; bottom transmittance b instead of 4b 0.007; 62 bright GaN bands taken as the pairs 0.032.

A check in Python, standard library only:

nH, nL, nT, nS, A, N = 2.4750, 2.2820, 2.1500, 1.4650, 0.00440, 63
b = (nL/nH)**(2*N); Tb = 4*b/(1 + b)**2
Y = (nS/nT)**20; Tt = 4*nH*Y/(nH + Y)**2
print(Tb/(Tb + Tt + A))   # 0.02725
