Processing of MIR319 and MIR159 precursors starts with a cut in the loop and works down the stem, so the loop-proximal duplex is released first, and its 3' arm strand is the entry asked for. The tempting wrong answer is MIMAT0000511, ath-miR319a-3p.1, the abundant mature miR319a: that strand is the 3' arm of the duplex released last, which is what the usual base-first mechanism would put first. The 5' arm strand of the first duplex is MIMAT0060847, and the stem-loop itself is MI0000544.
