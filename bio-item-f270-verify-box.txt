By hand, CYP27A1 oxidises the side chain of 5beta-cholestane-3alpha,7alpha-diol stereospecifically to (25R)-3alpha,7alpha-dihydroxy-5beta-cholestan-26-oic acid, C27H46O4 (Hoshita et al. 1978). The microsomal P450 whose loss removes cholic acid is CYP8B1, the 12alpha-hydroxylase (Li-Hawkins et al. 2002); one added oxygen (15.995 Da) gives the (25R)-3alpha,7alpha,12alpha-trihydroxy acid, C27H46O5 (Wang et al. 2024). Rhea lists this reaction as master RHEA:82331 with directed forms 82332 to 82334.
