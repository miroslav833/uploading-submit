Key: 63 m. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY: z_max = 63.413 m" of the first script, with "numerical time integration: 63.413 m" below it. By hand: Kajikawa, J. Meteor. Soc. Japan 53 (1975) 267-275, Table 1 on p. 273, gives v = 225 D^0.600 (cm/s, melted diameter D in cm) for ordinary dendritic and fernlike crystals. The crystal rises at w - v while m = (pi/6) D^3 grows at 1.50e-8 g/s, so dt = (pi/(2 mdot)) D^2 dD; it starts at v = 15.84 cm/s and stops rising at v = w = 30.0 cm/s, D* = (30/225)^(1/0.6) = 0.03480 cm. Then z = (pi/(2 mdot)) [w (D*^3 - D0^3)/3 - 225 (D*^3.6 - D0^3.6)/3.6] = 1.0472e8 x (4.0412e-4 - 3.4356e-4) = 6341 cm = 63 m. The tempting route sets the same integral up with a fall-speed law guessed from general knowledge of dendrites; a law such as 45 d^0.26 (d in mm) gives 4.1 m, and the other rows of the table give 22 to 440 m.
