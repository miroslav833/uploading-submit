Key: C5316

The NCI Thesaurus lists the concept "Borderline Breast Phyllodes Tumor" under the code C5316 (EVS browser https://evsexplore.semantics.cancer.gov, concept C5316, NCIt version 26.10d). Its parents are C7503 Borderline Phyllodes Tumor and C7575 Breast Phyllodes Tumor, and it has no children. The attached notes reproduce these rows twice, from NCI's EVS REST API and from EBI's OLS4 copy of NCIt (version 26.02d), and the two copies agree.

Working by hand, leaf-like stromal fronds with a two-layered epithelial lining and condensed stroma under the epithelium make a phyllodes tumour. For the WHO 5th edition grade, the edge is focally permeative, cellularity is moderate and atypia mild to moderate. The mitotic count is 8 per 10 fields of 0.50 mm, so 8/1.963 mm2 = 4.07 per mm2, inside the borderline band of 2.5 to below 5 per mm2. There is no stromal overgrowth and no malignant heterologous element, so the grade is borderline. The most specific concept with grade and organ is Borderline Breast Phyllodes Tumor, C5316.

The most tempting wrong answer is C7503 (Borderline Phyllodes Tumor), the right grade without the organ, which is the parent of C5316. C4504 and C5196 are the breast concepts of the malignant and benign grades, and C189340 (Childhood Breast Phyllodes Tumor) carries no grade.

This short Python program (standard library) checks the key.

import math
mitoses = 8 / (10 * math.pi * 0.25**2)            # per mm2, field diameter 0.50 mm
f = {"border": "focally permeative", "cellularity": "moderate", "atypia": "mild to moderate",
     "overgrowth": False, "heterologous": False}
malignant = f["heterologous"] or (f["border"] == "permeative" and f["cellularity"] == "marked"
            and f["atypia"] == "marked" and mitoses >= 5 and f["overgrowth"])
benign = (f["border"] == "well defined" and f["cellularity"] == "mild" and f["atypia"] in ("none", "mild")
          and mitoses < 2.5 and not f["overgrowth"])
grade = "Malignant" if malignant else ("Benign" if benign else "Borderline")
codes = {"Benign": "C5196", "Borderline": "C5316", "Malignant": "C4504"}   # <grade> Breast Phyllodes Tumor
print(round(mitoses, 2), grade, codes[grade])      # 4.07 Borderline C5316

Sources are Tan et al., Histopathology 77, 181 (2020); Tan et al., Histopathology 68, 5 (2016); and Sioutos et al., J. Biomed. Inform. 40, 30 (2007).
