Key: 260 m (260.9 m) least distance from the umbilical crossing to the touchdown point. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY linear rule D = 260.9 m" of key.py and "first touchdown distance that clears = 261.0 m" (0.1 m steps) of independent.py. DNV-OS-H203 (February 2012) Sec.4 C503, page 23: "Between lines and pipelines linear relationship between vertical and horizontal clearances should be assumed" (Figure 4-1, "No line area"), with Table 4-3 (page 24) V = 20 m, H = 100 m. a = 1500/1.23 = 1219.5 m; boundary slope along the line m = 20 sin 30/100 = 0.1; tangency sinh(u/a) = 0.1 at u = 121.7 m, z = 6.08 m; D = (20 + 0.1 x 121.7 - 6.08)/0.1 = 260.9 m. The most tempting wrong answers: 420 m (rectangular no-line area) and 220 m (both clearances at the ends only). Check: import math; a=1500/1.23; u=a*math.asinh(0.1); print(round((20+0.1*u-a*(math.sqrt(1.01)-1))/0.1,1)) prints 260.9. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-02/OS-H203.pdf, pages 23 and 24.
