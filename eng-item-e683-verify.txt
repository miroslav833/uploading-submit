EurOtop (2018) Sec. 5.4.7 and Fig. 5.44: for a wall on top of a 1:6 slope with breaking waves gamma_v = 0.92, fixed, with tan(alpha) = 1/6 (Van Doorslaer, De Rouck and Van der Meer, ICCE 2016). q/sqrt(g H^3) = 1.1288e-4; prefactor 0.026 x 0.8835/sqrt(1/6) = 0.05627; (ln(0.05627/1.1288e-4))^(1/1.3) = 4.0753; R_c = 4.0753 x 0.8835 x 2.00 x 0.92/2.5 = 2.650 m; h_wall = 2.650 - 1.80 = 0.85 m. The most tempting wrong answer is 0.65 m: the factor exp(-0.56 h_wall/R_c) (Eq. 5.45), solved with R_c = 1.80 + h_wall, gives 0.673 m; that factor was fitted to non-breaking waves on 1:2 and 1:3 slopes, and the manual gives the fixed 0.92 for this case. No factor gives 1.10 m; 0.65 gives 0.07 m.
