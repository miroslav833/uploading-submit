Key: 14.1 mm. It stands in the attached notes in the first section (output line 'KEY D = 14.133 mm') and in the second section (bore-by-bore classification, 14.13 mm). By hand, with Ishiguro and Iguchi, ISIJ International 43 (5), 663-670 (2003), Eq. (10): j_w - j_g = 0.165 (sigma/(rho_g D))^(1/2), j = 4Q/(pi D^2); inverted annular flow is on the side of larger j_w - j_g (Figs. 18, 19). For fixed flows j_w - j_g falls as D^-2 and the boundary as D^-1/2, so the largest bore is on the boundary: D^(3/2) = 4 (Q_w - Q_g)/(0.165 pi sqrt(sigma/rho_g)) = 4 x 5.3333e-5/(0.165 pi x 0.24495) = 1.6802e-3 m^1.5, D = 0.014133 m.
