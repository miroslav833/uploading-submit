Key depends on the plot. Method: Halcrow, Chem. Soc. Rev. 42, 1784 (2013), section "Dynamic and disordered Jahn-Teller distortions", Fig. 2.

Read from the figure:
Cu-N1 2.031, Cu-N2 2.140, Cu-N3 2.343 A
U, largest first (A^2): Cu 0.0195 0.0195 0.0195; N1 0.0420 0.0388 0.0197; N2 0.0538 0.0352 0.0318; N3 0.0538 0.0365 0.0331
N2 and N3 ellipsoids long along their bonds -> 0.0538 is the bond component. N1 long across -> 0.0197.

dU along bond: N1 0.0002, N2 0.0343, N3 0.0343. N1 ordered, N2 and N3 each two sites.
Long axis on N3 (weight p) or N2 (1 - p), never N1. d_s = 2.031.
d_l = 2.140 + 2.343 - 2.031 = 2.452 -> 2.45 A. p = 0.741.
Check: p(1-p)(d_l - d_s)^2 = 0.0340 vs 0.0341 observed.

Most tempting wrong answer 2.53: assumes 50:50, 2.343 + sqrt(0.0341). Plotted 2.343 taken as real: 2.34.
No libration correction (stem says so); it would add about 0.006 A.
