Key: 9.9 h. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3 (tally in 0.1 h steps). By hand: Ma et al., Front. Plant Sci. 16, 1525022 (2025), sections 3.1.1 and 3.1.2.2: in both Berchemia the pollen shed inside the petal tube is pushed out through the cleft at its top by the filament and anther (secondary pollen presentation) before the anther emerges, for about 7.6 h in B. flavescens and 2.7 h in B. polyphylla var. leioclada; the emerged anthers then present pollen for 63.5 h and 58.5 h. Offer from cleft to anthers over the stigma: 75.1 - 4.0 = 71.1 h and 67.7 - 6.5 = 61.2 h; difference 9.9 h. The most tempting wrong answer is 5.0 h, counting only from the emergence of the anther (63.5 - 58.5). Check in Python: print(round((75.1-4.0)-(67.7-6.5),1)) prints 9.9. Citation: Ma, Zhao, Tian, Fu and Liu, Front. Plant Sci. 16, 1525022 (2025), sections 3.1.1 and 3.1.2.2.
