Key: 2.7 m (2.675 m) smallest GM. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY GM_min = 2.6748 m" of key.py and "smallest GM = 2.675 m" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H204 (November 2013) Sec.3 [4.2.1] guidance note, page 18: before jacket sliding the range of static stability shall be not less than (15 + 10/GM) degrees. The range ends where KG = KN/sin(phi); with KN linear between 15 deg (5.45 m) and 20 deg (6.55 m), KG = 19.525 m gives a range of 18.74 deg and GM = 22.20 - 19.525 = 2.675 m, whose requirement is 15 + 10/2.675 = 18.74 deg. The most tempting wrong answer, 3.0 m, uses a flat 20 deg: KG = 6.55/sin 20 = 19.15 m. Check: import math; f=lambda K: (lambda p: (5.45+0.22*(p-15))/math.sin(math.radians(p))-K)(15+10/(22.2-K)); print(22.2-19.525, f(19.525)) prints 2.675 and a residual near 0.
