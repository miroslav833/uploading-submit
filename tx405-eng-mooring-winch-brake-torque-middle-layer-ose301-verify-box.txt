Key: 3.2e3 kN m (3178.1 kN m). It stands in the attached notes in the section 'Key' (line 'KEY 3178.1 kN m -> 3.2e3 kN m (2 s.f.)') and in the section 'Independent route' (line 'r = 1.0940 m, M = 3.1781e+06 N m = 3178.1 kN m'). Source: DNV-OS-E301 (October 2010), Ch.2 Sec.4 K504, page 76, free at rules.dnv.com: a winch with a stopper device needs an independent brake of at least 50 % of the anchor line breaking strength, and 'For winches the middle layer shall be used as reference.' By hand: brake force 0.50 x 5810 = 2905 kN. Layers hold 30 pi D_k, D_k = 1.600 + (2k - 1) 0.084 m; 6 layers hold 1189.8 m, 7 hold 1443.5 m, so 1350 m needs 7 and the middle layer is layer 4, D_4 = 2.188 m, r = 1.094 m. Torque 2905 x 1.094 = 3178.1 kN m = 3.2e3 kN m. Most tempting wrong answer: 3.9e3 kN m, the outermost layer (D_7 = 2.692 m, r = 1.346 m, 2905 x 1.346 = 3910 kN m) taken as the worst case; the standard refers the brake to the middle layer.
