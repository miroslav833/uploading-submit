Key: 5.74 mm (5.7398 mm) from the coxa-trochanter joint to the tibia tip at capture. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY reach = ... = 5.7398 mm" of key.py and the constant "distance 5.7398 mm" at every sweep angle of independent.py. Bäumler, Gorb and Büsse (2026), Front. Zool., Results, hunting sequence (c): in the sweep of H. orlovi "the body-coxa and femur-tibia joints do not change opening position, the coxa-trochanter joint opens" to impale the prey, so the right angle set in the approach holds at capture: sqrt(5.20^2 + 2.43^2) = 5.7398 mm. The most tempting wrong answers come from the grasping strike of other mantises: 2.77 mm (tibia folded) or 7.63 mm (leg extended). Check: print(round((5.20**2+2.43**2)**0.5,4)) prints 5.7398.
