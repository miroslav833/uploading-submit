Source: Kuhn, Peterson and Levin, NACA TN 2661 (1952), open on the NASA Technical Reports Server. Section 4.11(b), p. 47: for single uprights "the stress sigma_U should not exceed the column yield stress, and the average stress over the cross section of the upright, sigma_Uav = sigma_U A_Ue/A_U (38), should not exceed the allowable stress for a column with the slenderness ratio h_U/2rho". Section 4.1(b), p. 41: A_Ue = A_U/[1 + (e/rho)^2]. Formulas (27), (30a), (30c), (30e) on pp. 18-20.

By hand: (e/rho)^2 = 0.9103, A_Ue = 0.02534 in2, A_Ue/A_U = 0.5235, A_Ue/(d t) = 0.06598. h_U/(2 rho) = 114.8, Euler stress pi^2 x 10600/114.8^2 = 7.939 ksi, so sigma_U at the limit = 7.939/0.5235 = 15.17 ksi (below 40 ksi). import math
E,mu,he,hU,d,t,AU,e,rho,tcr=10600,0.32,50,45,12,0.032,0.0484,0.187,0.196,0.6
Ae=AU/(1+(e/rho)**2); allow=math.pi**2*E/(hU/(2*rho))**2
def avg(tau):
    k=math.tanh(0.5*math.log10(tau/tcr)); B=1+mu+k*(1-mu); a=0.7
    for i in range(300):
        s=k*math.tan(a)/(Ae/(d*t)+0.5*(1-k)); a=0.5*(a+math.atan(math.sqrt(B/(B+s))))
    return s*tau*Ae/AU
lo,hi=1.0,30.0
for i in range(100):
    m=(lo+hi)/2
    if avg(m)<allow: lo=m
    else: hi=m
print(round(lo*he*t,2))   # 17.10
