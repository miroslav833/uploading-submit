Key: 36 m (35.79 m). It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and in section 3 (line SAME KEY BY THIS ROUTE). Source: DNV-RP-E303 (2005), Annex A, page 20, Eq. (A-3), printed as du_a = N_c * s_u,tip^LB + A_inside * alpha * s_u,D^av / A_in, where s_u,tip^LB is '2/3 of the average of compression, extension and DSS shear strengths at skirt tip level. The factor 2/3 should be looked upon as a safety factor of 1.5 with respect to achieving full penetration when specifying the allowable underpressure.' The second (inside friction) term has no factor. Eqs. (A-1), (A-2) on pages 19-20; alpha 'normally assumed equal to the inverse of the sensitivity'. By hand: D_i = 4.940 m, A_in = 19.167 m2, A_tip = 0.46841 m2, alpha = 0.40, s_avD = 2.0 + 0.75z, average tip strength 1.9667 + 1.50z. Setting du_n = du_a and multiplying by A_in, the inside friction cancels: pi(5.00)(0.40)z(2.0 + 0.75z) + 0.46841[7.6(1.9667 + 1.5z) + 6z] - 1380 - (2/3)(7.6)(19.167)(1.9667 + 1.5z) = 0, i.e. 4.7124z^2 - 124.95z - 1563.98 = 0, z = 35.79 m; both underpressures 616.3 kPa there. Most tempting wrong answer: dividing the whole allowable underpressure (tip and friction terms) by 1.5 gives 28 m; Eq. (A-3) puts the 2/3 on the tip strength only. Check program (Python, standard library):
import math
Di=4.94; Ain=math.pi*Di**2/4; At=math.pi*(25-Di**2)/4; a0=5.9/3; f=2/3*7.6*Ain
A=math.pi*5*0.4*0.75; B=math.pi*5*0.4*2+At*(7.6*1.5+6)-f*1.5; C=At*7.6*a0-1380-f*a0
print((-B+math.sqrt(B*B-4*A*C))/(2*A))   # 35.79 m
