By hand, with y = 2/T - 1 = (1 + R)/(1 - R): two lossless scatterers a distance d apart give y_ab = y_a y_b - sqrt((y_a^2 - 1)(y_b^2 - 1)) cos(2 beta d + const). A spacing that is uniform over half a guide wavelength makes the phase uniform over 2 pi, so cos averages to 0 and cos^2 to 1/2. Then <y_ab> = y_a y_b and <y_ab^2> = y_a^2 y_b^2 + (y_a^2 - 1)(y_b^2 - 1)/2, which is the same as saying that (3 y^2 - 1)/2 multiplies on average. For one screw y = g = 1.05255/0.94745 = 1.11093 and (3 g^2 - 1)/2 = 1.35125. For 83 screws <y> = g^83 = 6194 and <y^2> = 1/3 + (2/3) x 1.35125^83 = 1/3 + (2/3) x 7.095e10 = 4.730e10. The variance of y is 4.730e10 - 6194^2 = 4.726e10, its standard deviation 2.174e5, and R/T = (y - 1)/2, so the standard deviation of R/T is 1.087e5, which is 1.1 x 10^5 to two figures. One screw more or less gives 1.26e5 or 9.35e4.

The wrong answer that tempts most is 2.2 x 10^5, the standard deviation of 2/T - 1 with the factor 1/2 forgotten. The mean of R/T is 3097 and is sometimes given in place of the spread. If the constant 1/3 and the factor 2/3 are dropped one gets 1.3 x 10^5.

A check in Python, standard library only. It prints the closed form and then the same standard deviation for three screws by brute-force quadrature over the two spacings.

import cmath, math
R1, N = 0.05255, 83
g = (1 + R1)/(1 - R1); P2 = (3*g*g - 1)/2
sd = lambda n: 0.5*math.sqrt(1/3 + (2/3)*P2**n - g**(2*n))
print(sd(N))                             # 108699
b = 2*math.sqrt(R1/(1 - R1))             # shunt susceptance of one screw
S = ((1 - 0.5j*b, -0.5j*b), (0.5j*b, 1 + 0.5j*b))
mul = lambda A, B: tuple(tuple(sum(A[i][k]*B[k][j] for k in range(2)) for j in range(2)) for i in range(2))
v, n = [], 64
for i in range(n):
    for j in range(n):
        M = S
        for p in (i, j):
            ph = 2*math.pi*(0.30 + 0.5*(p + 0.5)/n)
            M = mul(mul(M, ((cmath.exp(-1j*ph), 0), (0, cmath.exp(1j*ph)))), S)
        v.append(abs(M[1][1])**2 - 1)
m = sum(v)/len(v)
print(math.sqrt(sum((x - m)**2 for x in v)/len(v)), sd(3))   # both 0.156763
