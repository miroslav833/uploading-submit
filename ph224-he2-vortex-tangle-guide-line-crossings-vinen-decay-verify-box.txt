By hand. Total guide length 44.00 mm, slab 0.700 mm, so L0 = 2N/(l d) = 2 x 236 / (44.00e-3 m x 0.250e-3 m) = 4.2909e7 per m^2. kappa = h/m4 = 9.9693e-8 m^2/s, beta = chi_2 kappa/2pi = 4.7600e-9 m^2/s. Free decay 1/L = 1/L0 + beta t, so L0/10 is reached at t = 9/(beta L0) = 9/0.20425 per s = 44.06 s, i.e. 44 s. Counts 234 to 239 give 44 s; 233 gives 45 s and 240 gives 43 s.

The most tempting wrong answer is 88 s, from L0 = N/(l d) without the factor 2. With pi/2 or 4/pi instead of 2: 56 or 69 s; exponential decay: 11 s.

A check in Python, standard library only:

import math
kappa = 6.62607015e-34/6.6464731e-27
L0 = 2*236/(44.00e-3*0.250e-3)
beta = 0.300*kappa/(2*math.pi)
print(L0, 9/(beta*L0))   # 4.2909e7, 44.06
