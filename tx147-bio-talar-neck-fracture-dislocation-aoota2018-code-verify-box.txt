Key: 81.2.D(b). The key is in the first section of the attached notes (output line KEY) and again in the second section (line code).
By hand, from the page for bone 81 of the 2018 compendium (J Orthop Trauma 32 Suppl 1, section Foot, S90 and S91). The talus is 81. Body is 1, neck is 2 and head is 3, written between dots. The fracture line comes out in front of the lateral process, so the location is 81.2. The neck types are 81.2.A nondisplaced (Hawkins 1), 81.2.B displaced with subtalar subluxation or dislocation (Hawkins 2), 81.2.C displaced with talar body dislocation (Hawkins 3) and 81.2.D displaced with talar body and head dislocation (Hawkins 4). Here the body is out of the ankle joint and out of the subtalar joint, and the head is out of the talonavicular joint, so the type is D. Types B, C and D take the qualification a for simple or b for multifragmentary. Three neck pieces give b, in round brackets. Universal modifiers are left out because the question says so.
Wrong answers that tempt most. 81.2.C(b) comes from counting the body dislocation and missing the head. 81.2C(d) comes from taking the type letter from the fragmentation, as for a shaft, and the Hawkins type as the qualification. 81.2D2 or 81B3 come from the long-bone layout with a numbered group.
Check in seconds (Python, standard library):
body_out, subtalar_out, head_out, pieces = True, True, True, 3
t = 'D' if body_out and head_out else 'C' if body_out else 'B' if subtalar_out else 'A'
print('81.2.' + t + ('' if t == 'A' else '(b)' if pieces else '(a)'))   # 81.2.D(b)
