By hand: Darcy flow u = -(b^2/(12 mu)) grad(p - rho Omega^2 r^2/2), rim r = R + zeta e^(i n theta + sigma t), Laplace pressure from the in-plane curvature. sigma_n = (b^2 n/(12 mu R)) [rho Omega^2 R - gamma (n^2 - 1)/R^2]. The fastest mode has rho Omega^2 R^3 = gamma (3n^2 - 1), so sigma_max = b^2 gamma n^3/(6 mu R^3) and the rotation rate drops out. With n = 57: 6 mu R^3 = 6.644e-4, b^2 gamma = 1.890e-9, n^3 = 185193, sigma_max = 0.5268 1/s, e-folding time 1.898 s, so 1.9 s.
