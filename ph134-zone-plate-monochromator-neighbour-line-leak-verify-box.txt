Key by hand. For a plane wave of wavelength lam the Fresnel integral on the axis at the distance f, with u = r^2/(lam0 f) and x = lam0/lam, is U = -i pi x int T(u) exp(i pi x u) du, so a zone k with transmission c adds -c [exp(i pi x k) - exp(i pi x (k-1))]. The nickel zone 2j-1 (transmission tau) and the bare zone 2j together add -(1 - exp(-i pi x)) (1 + tau exp(-i pi x)) exp(2 pi i x j), and the sum over j = 1 to M = 51 gives I/I_inc = sin^2(pi M x)/cos^2(pi x/2) x |1 + tau exp(-i pi x)|^2. tau = exp(-2 pi beta t/lam) exp(-2 pi i delta t/lam) with t = 97 nm: |tau| = 0.7611 and phase -1.1559 rad at 2.4781 nm, |tau| = 0.6846 and phase -1.3486 rad at 2.8787 nm. Design line, x = 1: 4 M^2 |1 - tau_0|^2 = 4 x 2601 x 0.9657 = 10047. Second line, x = 0.86084: M x = 43.9028, sin^2 = 0.0903, cos^2(pi x/2) = 0.0470, first factor 1.920; |1 + tau_1 exp(-i pi x)|^2 = 1 + 0.4687 + 2 x 0.6846 x cos(2.7044 + 1.3486) = 0.6299; intensity 1.210. Ratio 1.210/10047 = 1.204e-4. Answer 1.2e-4.

The most tempting wrong answer is 1.6e-4: the plate treated as a lens for its first diffraction order alone, |1 - tau_1|^2/|1 - tau_0|^2 x^2 (sin p/p)^2 with p = pi M (x - 1) = -22.30, which leaves out the undiffracted wave and the other orders although they are as strong as the first order this far from its focus; dropping the factor 1/lam in front of the Fresnel integral for the second line gives the same number. Other values: the second line taken as unfocused, with its incident intensity at the pinhole, 1/10047 = 1.0e-4; nickel taken as opaque 1.8e-4; central zone taken as bare, or the phase of nickel taken with the wrong sign, 3.4e-4; material factor left out for the second line only 1.9e-4; sin^2 replaced by its mean 1/2, 6.7e-4; the number of zones 102 put in place of M, 1.1e-4; 50, 52, 49 or 53 bare rings 2.4e-5, 5.9e-4, 4.2e-4 or 1.1e-3; centre of the rings taken at the left edge of the image, (654.3/95.8)^2 = 47 zones, or 56 zones with the first radius read a little smaller; 28 bare rings give 4.5e-4. The first order alone with a geometric blur disc in the plane of the pinhole, |1 - tau_1|^2/(|1 - tau_0|^2 pi^2 102^2 (lam1/lam0 - 1)^2), also gives 4.5e-4.

Check in a second (Python 3, standard library):
import cmath, math
M, t, l0, l1 = 51, 97.0, 2.4781, 2.8787
tau = lambda d, b, l: cmath.exp(2j*math.pi*(-d + 1j*b)*t/l)
def I(x, tz):                                  # sum over the 102 zones, zone 1 nickel
    return abs(sum((tz if k % 2 else 1)*(cmath.exp(1j*math.pi*x*k) - cmath.exp(1j*math.pi*x*(k-1))) for k in range(1, 2*M+1)))**2
I0, I1 = I(1.0, tau(4.70e-3, 1.11e-3, l0)), I(l0/l1, tau(6.37e-3, 1.79e-3, l1))
print(I0, I1, I1/I0)                           # 10047.3 1.2097 1.204e-4
p = math.pi*M*(l0/l1 - 1)
print(abs(1 - tau(6.37e-3, 1.79e-3, l1))**2/abs(1 - tau(4.70e-3, 1.11e-3, l0))**2*(l0/l1)**2*(math.sin(p)/p)**2)   # 1.63e-4, first order alone
