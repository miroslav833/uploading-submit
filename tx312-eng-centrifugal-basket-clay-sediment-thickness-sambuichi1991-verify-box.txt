Key: 21.3 mm. It stands in the attached notes in the first section (output line 'KEY cake thickness = 21.3056 mm') and again in the second section (Runge-Kutta shooting, 21.3056 mm). By hand, with Sambuichi, Nakakura and Osasa, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 24 (4), 489-494 (1991): Eq. (7), dp_s/dr = (rho_p - rho)(1 - eps) r omega^2, and the Fig. 3 fit for Hara Gairome clay, eps = 0.86 - 9.74e-3 p_s^0.285 (Pa). omega = 2 pi 45.0 = 282.74 rad/s, drho omega^2 = 1603 x 282.74^2 = 1.2815e8 Pa/m2. Since (1 - eps) r dr = dp_s/(drho omega^2), the solids balance W = 2 pi h rho_p Int (1 - eps) r dr gives the wall pressure p_0 = drho omega^2 W/(2 pi h rho_p) = 78445 Pa. Also r_0^2 - r_d^2 = (2/(drho omega^2)) Int_0^p0 dp/(0.14 + 9.74e-3 p^0.285) = 2 x 2.4395e5/1.2815e8 = 3.8072e-3 m2, so r_d = 0.07869 m and the cake is 21.3 mm thick. The most tempting wrong answer is 17.6 mm: it takes the porosity at the wall pressure (0.618) for the whole cake.
