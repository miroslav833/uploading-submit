The mRNA and the gel were made for this question, so the answer is checked from the rungs themselves. Rungs are counted from the top, below the run-off band. As mRNA (lane G = C, A = U, T = A, C = G) rungs 1 to 92 read GAUACAUGCUAAUCACUUAAGGAGGUCAUUACAUGUCAAACGACAUUUGGCAGGUCCGUCUGCGCACUGGAUUCGAACACGCUAAGUACCCG. GGAGG is at rungs 21 to 25 and the start codon at rungs 33 to 35. Bands in the translation lanes: rung 58 (Mup only), rung 76 (Ery only), rung 84 (all three, a pause). First Ile codon AUU = codon 5, rungs 45 to 47. With mupirocin it is in the A site, codon 4 (rungs 42 to 44) is in the P site, and 58 - 42 = 16. For erythromycin 76 - 16 = 60, the first base of codon 10 (CUG), so the answer is codons 1 to 10, MSNDIWQVRL. The most tempting wrong answer is MSNDIWQVRLR: with the Ile codon in the P site the distance is 58 - 45 = 13 and 76 - 13 = 63 is the first base of codon 11 (CGC, Arg), one residue too many. Check in Python: s='GAUACAUGCUAAUCACUUAAGGAGGUCAUUACAUGUCAAACGACAUUUGGCAGGUCCGUCUGCGCACUGGAUUCGAACACGCUAAGUACCCG'; a=s.find('AUG',s.find('GGAGG')); print(a+1, [s[i:i+3] for i in range(a,a+33,3)], (76-(58-(a+1+9))-(a+1))//3+1) prints 33, the codons AUG UCA AAC GAC AUU UGG CAG GUC CGU CUG CGC and 10.
