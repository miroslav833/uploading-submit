Key: 49 cm (48.96 cm). It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3 (RK4 integration of the balances). By hand: Tojo, Miyanami and Yano, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 8, 122 (1975): K_c a_0 = 0.015 u_d (Eq. (4)); E_Ka = 1 + 0.34 (A nu)^1.6 with A nu in cm/s (Eq. (5)); plug flow, K_c a L/u_c = [1/(gamma - 1)] ln[(1 - X_out)/(1 - gamma X_out)], gamma = u_c/(m u_d), X = m x/y_in (Eq. (3), nomenclature). A nu = 0.500 x 4.00 = 2.00 cm/s, E_Ka = 2.031, K_c a = 0.015 x 0.150 x 2.031 = 0.00457 1/s; gamma = 3.205, X_out = 0.900/3.205 = 0.2808, transfer units ln(7.192)/2.205 = 0.8947; L = 0.250 x 0.8947/0.00457 = 49.0 cm. The most tempting wrong answer is 99 cm, without the vibration factor; a typical coefficient of 0.01 to 0.02 1/s gives 11 to 22 cm. Check in Python: import math; g=0.25/(0.52*0.15); X=0.9/g; print(round(0.25*math.log((1-X)/(1-g*X))/(g-1)/(0.015*0.15*(1+0.34*2.0**1.6)),2)) prints 48.96. Citation: Tojo, Miyanami and Yano, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 8, 122-126 (1975), Eqs. (3) to (5).
