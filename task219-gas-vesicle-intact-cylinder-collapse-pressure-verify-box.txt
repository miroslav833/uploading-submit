Measured from the pixels of the figure (scale bar 224 px = 100 nm): width of the cylindrical part 55.8 nm, the same for the three vesicles (the drawn width is 57.0 nm). Stain exclusion across the cylinder, averaged along it, relative to the axis: 0.71 at 0.7 of the half-width and 0.49 at 0.88. A full cylinder excludes stain along its chord, sqrt(1 - x^2) = 0.71 and 0.48; a flattened two-wall ribbon would give 1.0 and 1.0. So the vesicles are intact and r = W/2 = 28.5 nm. p_c = E t^3 / [4 (1 - nu^2) r^3] = 3.4e9 x 8 / (3.64 x 28.5^3) Pa = 0.32 MPa, key 0.3 (widths of 55 to 61 nm give 0.36 to 0.26). The most tempting wrong answer is 1 MPa: the usual correction for a vesicle flattened on drying, r = W/pi = 18.1 nm, gives 1.25 MPa. Check in Python: W = 57.0; f = lambda r: 3.4e9 * 2.0**3 / (3.64 * r**3) / 1e6; print(round(f(W / 2), 3), round(f(W / 3.14159), 3)) prints 0.323 1.251. Where to look: the gray level across any vesicle, brightest on the axis and falling toward the edges. Section 2 of the notes (readback_png.py) makes these measurements from the PNG.
