Key: BB40&XA3GE9&XN1VF. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3, built from the hierarchy depth and the BB40 axes. By hand: slow CO2-requiring Gram-negative rod, oxidase-positive, catalase-negative, no MacConkey growth, agar-pitting colonies with a bleach odour, in a needle-licking injector: Eikenella corrodens (Chen and Wilson, J. Periodontol. 63, 941, 1992), a HACEK organism. ICD-11 MMS 2025-01: BB40 Acute or subacute infectious endocarditis; anatomy XA7V72 Mitral valve has the leaf XA3GE9 Cusps of mitral valve (no per-leaflet code); the only agent code naming Eikenella is the genus leaf XN1VF. Cluster: BB40&XA3GE9&XN1VF. Most tempting wrong answer: BB40&XA7V72&XN1VF, coding the whole valve; reading the isolate as Kingella kingae gives BB40&XA3GE9&XN7EJ. Check in Python on the tabulation titles: rows=[('BB40','Acute or subacute infectious endocarditis'),('XA7V72','Mitral valve'),('XA3GE9','Cusps of mitral valve'),('XN7EJ','Kingella kingae'),('XN1VF','Eikenella')]; pick=lambda w:[c for c,t in rows if t==w][0]; print('&'.join([pick('Acute or subacute infectious endocarditis'),pick('Cusps of mitral valve'),pick('Eikenella')])) prints the key. Citations: Chen and Wilson (1992); Chambers et al., PLoS ONE 8, e63181 (2013); Harrison et al., BMC Med. Inform. Decis. Mak. 21, 206 (2021).
