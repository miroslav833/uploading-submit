(20) belongs to Eqs. (15) to (17) for moderate lengths. Here r/t = 64, l/r = 56.25, Z = 2.02e5, above the limit 11.8 (r/t)^2 (1 - mu^2) = 4.80e4, so the oval mode applies. p = 0.90 x 290000/(4 x 0.9936) x (1.250/80.0)^3 MPa = 250.51 kPa.
