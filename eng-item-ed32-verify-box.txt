Key: 38 m/s (37.90 m/s). It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3 (bisection). By hand: Miyahara, Terakado and Takahashi, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 16, 454 (1983), p. 457: pulsating/transient boundary H/D = 0.12 Fr*^1.23 Bo^0.8 for 0.55 < Bo < 4 (Eq. (4)) and H/D = Fr*^0.65 for 4 < Bo < 50, 1 < Fr* < 10 (Eq. (5)). Bo = 4.737, so Eq. (5): Fr* = (15.0/4.52)^(1/0.65) = 6.331; V_h = 6.331 x sqrt(973.795 x 9.81 x 4.52e-3/1.205) = 37.9 m/s. The most tempting wrong answer is 32 m/s, with Eq. (4) (Fr* = 5.405).
