Key: 29 m/s (29.04 m/s; line KEY of the attached notes, section 3, and section 4 by a second route). Takahashi et al., J. Chem. Eng. Japan 19, 456 (1986): holdup from Eq. (13), the Zivi minimum of kinetic-energy flux plus wall-friction dissipation W = 2 rho_l f_TP u_l^3 Z/D (Eqs. 10 to 12, p. 457). Re_l = 6742, f_TP = 0.0791 Re_l^-0.25 = 0.008729 (Eq. 15). At U_g = 29.04 m/s: holdup 0.08075, u_l = 1.863 m/s, zeta = 0.188 (Re_g Re_l)^0.1 = 1.398 (Eq. 19), dP1 + dP2 = 644.7 Pa, dP3 + dP4 + dP5 = 969.6 Pa, dP_t = 2000 Pa. The paper prints the liquid term of Eq. (13) with rho_l^3, a misprint; the derivative of the kinetic-energy flux gives rho_l^2, used here (the printed form gives 28.3 m/s). Above 12.73 m/s the holdup is below 0.1464, where the two-phase terms, zeta and the dry terms all rise with U_g, so the crossing at 29.04 m/s is the only one. Likely wrong answers: 20 m/s (classical Zivi slip without friction), 36 m/s (holdup minimising the two-phase pressure drop), 34 m/s (zeta left out).
