Key: 2.9 m (2.876 m) operational limiting Hs. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY eta 0.80 Hs = 2.876 m" of key.py and "largest Hs that passes = 2.875 m" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H206 (September 2014) [2.4.2.2], page 15: efficiency "shall generally not be taken higher than 80% of the theoretical operational values"; Guidance note 1: "if the heave comp system has 90% stated / documented efficiency, the maximum efficiency factor should be 0.9 x 0.9 ~ 0.8, or 80%", which is this case. Usable speed 0.80 x 1.43 = 1.144 m/s; design velocity per metre Hs = 2 pi x 0.160/8.0 x sqrt(2 ln 150) = 0.3978 m/s; 1.144/0.3978 = 2.876 m (stroke check 3.95 m; unrounded 0.81 gives 2.912 m, also 2.9). The most tempting wrong answer, 3.2 m, credits the documented 90 %. Check: import math; print(round(0.8*1.43/(2*math.pi*0.16/8*math.sqrt(2*math.log(150))),3)) prints 2.876. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-09/OS-H206.pdf, pages 15 and 37.
