Hand calculation. A beam interfering with its mirror image gives 2|l| petals, so |l| = 59. The absorbed orbital angular momentum flux is P l/omega with omega = 2 pi c/1064 nm = 1.7703e15 rad/s, so the torque is 31.80 uW x 59/omega = 1.0598e-18 N m. The spin part only spins the sphere about its centre, and there is no refractive-index factor. The force along the circle at r = 24.60 um is torque/r = 4.308e-14 N. The Faxen factor for parallel motion at a/h = 0.625 is 1/(1 - (9/16)x + x^3/8 - (45/256)x^4 - x^5/16) = 1.5476, so the drag coefficient is 6 pi (0.890e-3)(1.000e-6)(1.5476) = 2.596e-8 kg/s. Then v = 1.659 um/s and T = 2 pi r/v = 93.15 s, which rounds to 93 s.

The most tempting wrong answer misjudges the petal spacing instead of counting the petals. A spacing taken as 7.5 px gives 136 petals, l = 68 and 81 s, and 8 px gives 128 petals and 86 s. One petal pair fewer or more gives 95 s or 92 s. Taking l equal to the petal number gives 47 s, adding the spin (l + 1) gives 92 s, and leaving out the wall correction gives 60 s.

Check in Python:
import math
c=299792458; w=2*math.pi*c/1064e-9; a=1.0e-6; h=1.6e-6; r=24.6e-6
x=a/h; g=6*math.pi*0.890e-3*a/(1-9/16*x+x**3/8-45/256*x**4-x**5/16)
F=31.80e-6*59/(w*r)
print(2*math.pi*r*g/F)   # 93.15 s
