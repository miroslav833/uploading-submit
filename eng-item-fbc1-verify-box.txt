Key: 6.8 mm (6.77 mm) required plate thickness. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY 1.3*max(dp, 0.1 p_out) ... t= 6.769 mm -> 6.8" of key.py and "required 6.7695 mm -> 6.8 mm" of independent.py. By hand: p_out = 1025 x 9.81 x 30.0 = 301.66 kN/m2, p_in = 300.65 kN/m2, difference 1.006 kN/m2. DNV-OS-H102 (January 2012) Sec.5 B204, page 29: for counteracting and independent large hydrostatic pressures the load factor is applied to the pressure difference, which should not be taken less than 0.1 times the hydrostatic pressure. pd = 1.3 x max(1.006, 30.17) = 39.22 kN/m2; t = 15.8 x 1.0 x 0.850 x sqrt(39.22)/sqrt(308.70 x 0.5) = 6.77 mm, above the minimum 14.3 x 7/sqrt(308.70) = 5.70 mm. The most tempting wrong answer, 10 mm, factors each side on its own: 1.3 x 301.66 - 300.65 = 91.5 kN/m2, t = 10.3 mm. Check: import math; pd=1.3*max(1025*9.81*0.1/1000, 0.1*1025*9.81*30/1000); print(max(15.8*0.85*math.sqrt(pd)/math.sqrt(355/1.15*0.5), 14.3*7/math.sqrt(355/1.15))) prints 6.769. Sources: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-01/OS-H102.pdf (page 29) and https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2011-04/OS-C101.pdf (pages 38 and 39).
