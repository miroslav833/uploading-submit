The N-period row transmits fully where N beta = m pi; next to the stop band beta = pi(1 - 1/N). The phase derivative of t_N there is tau = N (d beta/d omega) |Im(1/t_1)|/sin(beta) = 4 N cos^2(pi/2N) [kappa - 2 cos^2(pi/2N)] T1/sin^2(pi/N). For N = 83: 4 x 83 x 0.999642 x 0.161431 x 1.3636 fs / 0.0014320 = 51.02 ps, so 51 ps. The product of the 165 layer matrices gives 51.015 ps at 1985.08 nm.
