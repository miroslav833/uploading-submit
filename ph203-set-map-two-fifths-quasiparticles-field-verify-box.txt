Key by hand. At 2/5 the quasiparticles carry e/5, so N_e = (2/5) N_phi + (N_qe - N_qh)/5 and B = (5/2) n h/e + (N_qh - N_qe) h/(2eA). (5/2) x 7.96443e14 x 4.135668e-15 = 8.23456 T; 13 x 4.135668e-15/(2 x 1.000e-12) = 0.02688 T; B = 8.26144 T. Answer 8.261 T.

Most tempting wrong answer. One flux quantum per excess quasihole, as at 1/3, gives 8.23456 + 13 x 4.1357e-3 = 8.288 T. Leaving out the quasiparticles gives 8.235 T.
