Key by hand. Jackson-Hunt: Delta T = K1 V lambda + K2/lambda, extremum at lambda^2 V = K2/K1. V = 1.118e-7/(1.073e9 x (2.1431e-6)^2) = 2.2687e-5 m/s = 22.7 um/s (undercooling 2 K2/lambda = 0.104 K).

Other values. Single lamella width taken as lambda: 90.7 um/s. Factor 2 dropped or added in the extremum: 11.3 or 45.4 um/s. 136 or 138 pairs: 22.4 or 23.0 um/s.
