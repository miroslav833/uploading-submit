Key: 2.76 m (2.7579 m) largest significant wave height. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY limiting Hs = 2.7579 m" of key.py and "largest Hs (0.1 mm steps) = 2.7578 m, first member exceeded: sling 2" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H206 (September 2014) [5.3.2.2], page 38: with forces from the [5.4] methods, the [5.3.1] criterion (Fhyd <= 0.9 Fstatic-min) applies to the main hoist line, "For individual slings Fhyd ≤ 1.0 ⋅ Fstatic-min is acceptable." Hoist line 0.9 x 1200.0/380.0 = 2.842 m; sling 2 at 1.0: 262.0/95.0 = 2.758 m, the lowest of all members, so sling 2 governs. The most tempting wrong answer, 2.48 m, applies the quoted 0.9 to sling 2; the hoist line alone gives 2.84 m. Check: print(round(min(0.9*1200/380, 310/107, 262/95, 298/101, 330/110),4)) prints 2.7579. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-09/OS-H206.pdf, page 38.
