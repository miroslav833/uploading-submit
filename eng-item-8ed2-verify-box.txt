Key: 9.69 m (9.687 m) least freeboard to the shaft tops. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY 3 % and 6 m: spare-buoyancy freeboard 9.687 m -> least freeboard 9.69 m" of key.py and "GBS with open shafts: 3 % and 6 m: deepest draught 140.313 m, freeboard 9.687 m" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H201 (April 2012) Sec.6 F102 guidance note, page 40: for large concrete gravity base structures with open shafts the minimum freeboard is normally 6 m and the spare buoyancy requirement may be reduced to 3 %. Shaft area 4 x pi x 10.0^2 = 1256.6 m2; volume to the shaft tops 286 000 + 1256.6 x 105.0 = 417 947 m3; A f >= 0.03 (417 947 - A f) gives f = 0.03 x 417 947/(1.03 x 1256.6) = 9.687 m, above 6 m. The most tempting wrong answer, 15.8 m, uses the general 5 % (15.84 m). Check: import math; A=4*math.pi*100; print(round(0.03*(286000+A*105)/(1.03*A),3)) prints 9.687. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-04/OS-H201.pdf, page 40.
