The figure is the only source of the edge direction. It is a simulated STEM image, 600 x 460 px, made for this question, at 3.00 px per angstrom (not printed). The spots form a centred rectangular lattice: the shortest spot-to-spot vectors are 8.24 px long at 38.9 and -66.9 degrees above the horizontal, and the rows with a spacing of 9.93 px (a_ZZ) run at -14.0 degrees, which is the zigzag direction; along the armchair direction, at 76.0 degrees, the spots repeat every 13.14 px (a_AC), and 13.14/9.93 = 1.323 = 4.38/3.31. The ribbon, about 321 px (107 angstrom) wide, crosses the frame from lower left to upper right; both edges are staircases that rise at 27.4 degrees above the horizontal, 41.4 degrees from the zigzag direction. Of the vectors m a_ZZ + n a_AC with m, n <= 3 this is 3 a_ZZ + 2 a_AC (tan = 2 x 4.38/(3 x 3.31) = 0.882, 41.42 degrees); 2 a_ZZ + a_AC is at 33.5 and a_ZZ + a_AC at 52.9 degrees. The step pattern of the edges repeats every 39.7 px (13.24 angstrom).

Key by hand. Across the ribbon the direction makes 41.42 degrees with the armchair axis: w_yy = cos^2/0.170 + sin^2/1.12 = 3.6987 per m_e. Completing the square gives E_n(k) = n^2 hbar^2 pi^2 w_yy/(2 W^2) + hbar^2 k^2/(2 m_t) with m_t = 0.170 x 1.12 x w_yy m_e = 0.7042 m_e (not 1/w_xx = 0.325 m_e). E2 - E1 = 3 x 3.2540 meV x 3.6987 = 36.11 meV for W = 10.75 nm. The second subband starts to fill at k* = sqrt(2 m_t (E2 - E1))/hbar = 8.169e8 per m, linear density n* = 2 k*/pi = 5.201e8 per m (spin 2). The leads hold the ribbon's electrochemical potential at zero, so Delta V = e n*/C + (E2 - E1)/e = 1.0822 + 0.0361 = 1.1183 V, which is 1.1 V; without the second term, 1.08 V, also 1.1 V.

The most tempting wrong answer is 0.58 V: the lattice taken as lined up with the frame, zigzag vertical, so that the edges at 27.4 degrees look like 2 a_ZZ + 3 a_AC. The lattice is turned by 14 degrees. Other routes: 0.77 V (mass along the ribbon taken as 1/w_xx), 0.93 V (zigzag and armchair interchanged), 1.3 V (E2 in place of E2 - E1 in the density), 0.58 V also from leaving out the spin degeneracy, 0.69 V (isotropic mass 0.436 m_e), 1.0 V (edge at 45 degrees). Other edge directions: 2 a_ZZ + a_AC 1.32 V, a_ZZ + a_AC 0.82 V, 3 a_ZZ + a_AC 1.53 V, zigzag edge 1.78 V, armchair edge 0.27 V.

Check program (Python 3, standard library):
import math
hb, me, e = 1.054571817e-34, 9.1093837015e-31, 1.602176634e-19
a = math.atan2(2*4.38, 3*3.31); wyy = math.cos(a)**2/0.170 + math.sin(a)**2/1.12
dE = 3*hb**2*math.pi**2*wyy/(2*me*(10.75e-9)**2); mt = 0.170*1.12*wyy*me
n = 2*math.sqrt(2*mt*dE)/hb/math.pi
print(e*n/77.0e-12 + dE/e)   # 1.1183 (V)

The verification notes give the same number from a numerical solution across the ribbon with the full mass tensor, a table over all edge directions and a script that reads the lattice and the edge direction from the image file.
