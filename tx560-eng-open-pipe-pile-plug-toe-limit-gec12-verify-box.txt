Key: 170 kips (nominal axial resistance of the plugged 18 in open-end pipe pile). In the attached notes it is the KEY line of key.py (168.76 kips). The deciding point: an open pile that cores the clay displaces less soil, so its outside adhesion is reduced. GEC 12 Vol. I (FHWA-NHI-16-009, 2016), Section 7.10.7, pp. 406-407, Eq. 7-90: Rn = 0.8 Ca As + 4.5 su Ap for open-end pipe piles in stiff clay with a plug carried down, required for open pipe piles under 24 in in predominantly cohesive soils. By hand: As = pi x 1.5 x 30.0 = 141.4 ft2; 0.8 x 1.00 x 141.4 = 113.1 kips; Ap = 1.767 ft2; 4.5 x 7.00 x 1.767 = 55.7 kips; Rn = 168.8 kips. The tempting answer is 200 kips (full adhesion with 4.5 su). Check program (standard library): import math; print(round(0.8*1.00*math.pi*1.5*30.0 + 4.5*7.00*math.pi*1.5**2/4, 2))  prints 168.76.
