The alpha subunit (HADHA) carries hydratase and dehydrogenase, the beta subunit (HADHB) the thiolase; hydratase 88 % and dehydrogenase 91 % show the alpha subunit intact, and with biallelic variants in one subunit gene the gene is HADHB. In Mondo (release 2026-11-03) that is mitochondrial trifunctional protein deficiency 2, MONDO:0958185, a child of MONDO:0012172 with no child of its own. The most tempting wrong answer is MONDO:0012173, the LCHAD deficiency term, which the 3-hydroxyacylcarnitines suggest until the fibroblast assays are read.
