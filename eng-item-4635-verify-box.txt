Key: 0.825 m/s (0.8250 m/s) largest current velocity. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY v_max = ... = 0.8250 m/s" of key.py and "largest current speed (0.1 mm/s steps) = 0.8250 m/s" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H206 (September 2014) [2.9.5.2], page 23: "ROV thruster capacity for time critical operations should be at least 30% higher than the maximum expected current force acting on the ROV and its umbilical." The ROV cannot wait for the current to ease, so the task cannot be paused and is time critical in the plain sense (the term is not defined further). F_cur = 0.615 x 14.70 v^2 = 9.0405 v^2 kN; 8.00 >= 1.30 x 9.0405 v^2 gives v = 0.8250 m/s. The most tempting wrong answer, 0.941 m/s, sets the current force equal to the full thrust. Check: print(round((8/(1.3*0.615*14.7))**0.5,4)) prints 0.825. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-09/OS-H206.pdf, page 23.
