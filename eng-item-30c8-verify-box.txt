Key: 69.4 kN (69.41 kN) characteristic horizontal positioning load. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY characteristic horizontal positioning load = 69.412 kN" of key.py and "characteristic positioning load = ... = 69.412 kN" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H206 (September 2014) [7.2.1.2], page 52: "Characteristic ULS positioning loads in vertical and horizontal direction should normally not be taken less than 3% of the installed object's submerged weight including added mass." 0.03 x (1578 + 75.0 x 9.81) = 0.03 x 2313.75 = 69.41 kN, above the 28.0 kN maximum tool force. The most tempting wrong answer is the tool force itself, 28.0 kN; leaving out the added mass gives 47.3 kN. Check: print(round(0.03*(1578+75.0*9.81),3)) prints 69.412. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-09/OS-H206.pdf, page 52.
