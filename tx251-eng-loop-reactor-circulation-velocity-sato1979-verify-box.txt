(5), p. 451: u = 0.744 u' Re^0.7/(250 + Re^0.7) [(r1^2 - r2^2)/rt^2]^-0.6, with Re = d_t u rho/mu (nomenclature, p. 453). By hand: r1 = 4.50, r2 = 1.00, rt = 5.00 cm, n = 1.200 1/s, sin 30 deg = 0.5, so u' = 4.18879 x 1.200 x 90.125/25 x 0.5 = 9.060 cm/s. Radius term 0.770^-0.6 = 1.1698; 0.744 x 9.060 x 1.1698 = 7.885 cm/s. Re = 10.00 cm x u / 0.0100 cm2/s = 1000 u (u in cm/s). Solve u = 7.885 (1000u)^0.7/(250 + (1000u)^0.7): substitution from 9.060 gives 5.535, 4.930, 4.779, 4.738, 4.726, 4.723, 4.722. At the root Re = 4722, Re^0.7 = 373.2, Reynolds term 0.5988, u = 7.885 x 0.5988 = 4.722 cm/s. Re is inside the paper's range 2.3e3 to 2.0e5. Most tempting wrong answer: 10.5 cm/s, from a screw-type model with tan(phi) in place of sin(phi) and no fitted corrections, 4.18879 x 1.200 x 3.605 x 0.57735; a solver without the paper lands there. Then 9.06 cm/s, the simple model of Eq. (2) without the corrections, and 5.54 cm/s, one pass of Eq. (5) with Re from the simple-model velocity. Check of my reading of Eq.
