Key depends on where the axon ends. The pale area inside the compact myelin holds two zones.

From the image file (scale bar 400 px = 200 nm):
outer edge of compact myelin 1122 nm, inner edge 862 nm (equal-area diameters)
faint ring, concentric: 564 nm
inside the ring: dark particles median 9.7 nm, 11.8 per 10^4 nm^2 (neurofilaments) + microtubule rings
between ring and myelin: particles median 17.0 nm, 5.3 per 10^4 nm^2 (ribosomes) + microtubule rings, no 10 nm dots

Band = enlarged inner tongue, oligodendrocyte cytoplasm. Axon = inside the ring.
g = 564 / 1122 = 0.503 -> 0.5. With the outer tongue crescent (0.6 % of area): 564 / 1127 = 0.500.
Likely wrong answer: whole pale disc as axon, 862 / 1122 = 0.77 -> 0.8.

Check, standard-library Python
print(round(564/1122, 1), round(862/1122, 1))
Output: 0.5 0.8

Sources: Snaidero et al. 2014, Cell 156, 277-290, p. 277 (inner tongue, channels reopen in adults).
