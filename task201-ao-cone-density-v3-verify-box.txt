Density = cones counted in the figure divided by the retinal area of the field, with angle converted by Bennett, Rudnicka and Edgar (1994), q = 0.01306 (AL - 1.82) mm per degree.

AL 27.4 mm: q = 0.01306 x 25.58 = 0.3341 mm per degree. Field 0.3341 mm square, 0.1116 mm^2.

Image 2000 x 2000 px over 60 arcmin, 33.3 px per arcmin. 5,458 cone centres inside the field; an automatic local-maximum count finds 5,485 (it catches edge cones too). Power-spectrum ring: centre spacing 29.1 px = 0.873 arcmin. Either way 1.51 to 1.52 cones per square arcmin.

5458 / 0.1116 = 48,900 per mm^2; 5485 / 0.1116 = 49,100; from spacing, 2 / (sqrt(3) x (4.86 um)^2) = 48,800. All 49,000 at two significant figures.

Most tempting wrong answer: emmetropic 0.291 mm per degree. Area (0.291)^2 = 0.0847 mm^2, 5458 / 0.0847 = 64,000 (65,000 at 0.290). Square lattice 1/s^2 gives 42,000.
