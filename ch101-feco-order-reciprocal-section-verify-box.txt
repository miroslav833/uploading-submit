The figure carries the peak heights and widths of the two kinds of reflection. Measured on the stored image (Gaussian fits to every spot): fundamentals A = 2.01e6 counts above background, sigma = 5.98 px; superlattice A = 1360 counts, sigma = 17.40 px; 200 px per 1/a, background about 55 counts. By hand: integrated ratio = (A_s/A_f)(sigma_s/sigma_f)^3 = 6.77e-4 x 24.6 = 0.0167 = S^2 (6.96/11.94)^2, so S = 0.22; 140 analyses of the image (fits, background-subtracted sums, 1D cuts, 100/70/50 percent size) give 0.215 to 0.224. Most tempting wrong answer 0.20 (fundamental centres read at 2.5e6, superlattice at the 1e3 tick, width ratio judged near 3.2); squared width ratio gives 0.13. Shull and Siegel, Phys. Rev. 75, 1008 (1949); Wilson, Proc. R. Soc. A 181, 360 (1943).
