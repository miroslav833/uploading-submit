Key: EH90.4&XA5ZE2&XK8G. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3, built from the hierarchy depth and the Reference Guide example. By hand: intact maroon non-blanching boggy skin with a blood-filled blister over a loaded heel, no slough or eschar, is a deep tissue pressure injury (Edsberg et al., J. Wound Ostomy Continence Nurs. 43, 585, 2016), ICD-11 MMS EH90.4 Suspected deep pressure-induced tissue damage, depth unknown. In the WHO tabulation 2025-01, Heel XA5HK0 has the leaf children XA5ZE2 Posterior surface of heel, XA1QH8 medial, XA3R99 lateral, XA2N02 plantar; laterality Left is XK8G (Right XK9K, Bilateral XK9J), as in the Reference Guide example GB51&XK8G&XN6P4. Cluster: EH90.4&XA5ZE2&XK8G. Most tempting wrong answer: EH90.1&XA5ZE2&XK8G, reading the blister as grade 2; the swap XK9K for left gives EH90.4&XA5ZE2&XK9K. Check in Python on the tabulation titles: rows=[('EH90.1','Pressure ulceration grade 2'),('EH90.4','Suspected deep pressure-induced tissue damage, depth unknown'),('XA5HK0','Heel'),('XA5ZE2','Posterior surface of heel'),('XA2N02','Plantar surface of heel'),('XK9K','Right'),('XK8G','Left')]; pick=lambda w: [c for c,t in rows if t.startswith(w)][0]; print('&'.join([pick('Suspected deep'),pick('Posterior surface of heel'),pick('Left')])) prints the key.
