Key by hand. A band step is a thickness step 2 l_c = lambda/(2 dn): dn = 131 x 1.0642 um/(2 x 2851.0 um) = 0.024449, n_o(1064.2 nm) = 1.67380 - 0.024449 = 1.649351. Type I in a negative crystal: sin^2 theta = (n_o(w)^-2 - n_o(2w)^-2)/(n_e(2w)^-2 - n_o(2w)^-2) = (0.367599 - 0.356938)/(0.368604 - 0.356938) = 0.91383, theta = 72.93 deg, so 73 degrees.
Most tempting wrong answers: a miscount (130 bands give 72.2 deg, 132 give 73.7 deg, 128 give 70.9 deg; more than 143 bands give no phase matching), a band step of l_c (no phase matching) or 4 l_c (42 deg), and a linearised numerator (71 deg).
