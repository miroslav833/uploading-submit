So the film has 109 bilayers and the period is 257.3/109 = 2.3606 nm, 11.46 planes of 0.2060 nm.

Key by hand. Composition on planes, c_j - c0 = A cos(beta j d), beta = 2 pi/Lambda. Diffusion potential of plane j: (f'' + 2 eta^2 Y)(c_j - c0) - 2 kappa (c_{j+1} - 2 c_j + c_{j-1})/d^2; rate of change M times its second difference over d^2. Each second difference gives -B^2 d^2 with B^2 = 2(1 - cos(beta d))/d^2, so dA/dt = R A, R = -D B^2 [1 + (2 eta^2 Y + 2 kappa B^2)/f'']. Numbers: beta d = 0.54832 rad, B^2 = 6.909e18 m^-2 (continuum 7.085e18), 2 eta^2 Y = 3.771e8, 2 kappa B^2 = 3.593e8, bracket 11.264e8 J/m^3 = 2.888 f'', effective interdiffusion coefficient 1.386e-22 m^2/s, R = -9.579e-4 per s. Intensity goes as A^2: I/I0 = exp(2 R t) = exp(-4.023) = 0.0179 after 2100 s, so 0.018. A time integration of the plane equations from a square wave gives 0.0179 as well.

Other values. 108 bilayers: 0.020. 110 bilayers: 0.016. The most tempting wrong answer is 0.016, from beta^2 in place of B^2. Coherency strain left out: 0.069. Gradient energy left out: 0.065. Both left out: 0.25. Amplitude ratio given for the intensity: 0.13.

Short check (Python 3, standard library):
from math import *
d, lam = 0.2060e-9, 257.3e-9/109
B2 = 2*(1 - cos(2*pi*d/lam))/d**2
R = -4.800e-23*B2*(1 + (2*0.02550**2*2.900e11 + 2*2.600e-11*B2)/3.900e8)
print(B2, R, exp(2*R*35.00*60))      # 6.909e18, -9.579e-4, 0.0179
