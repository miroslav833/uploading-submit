Key: 3.2 g/h, two significant figures. In the attached notes the first program prints "KEY: vapour throughput = 3.2060 g/h -> 3.2 g/h at two figures" from the paper's Eq. (3), and the second prints 3.2060 g/h from the dimensionless Eq. (8).

Source: Munakata and Matsuda, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 6, 58 (1973). Ranges (section 5): turbulent Eq. (6) for Re_G > 3000; laminar Eq. (7) for Re_G < 2000 and Kn_2 < 1e-2; slip to molecular flow Eq. (8) for Kn_2 > 1e-2, with Kn_2 at the top pressure. Eq. (8) is Knudsen's semi-empirical equation, their Eq. (3): (pi/4) D^2 G = [(pi/128) D^4 rho_av/(mu l) + (sqrt(2 pi)/6) sqrt(M/RT) (D^3/l) (1 + x)/(1 + 1.235 x)] (p1 - p2), x = (rho_av/mu) sqrt(RT/M) D, rho_av at (p1 + p2)/2.

By hand: mean speed sqrt(8RT/(pi M)) = 175.5 m/s; top density 2.810e-4 kg/m3; mean free path 2 mu/(rho c) = 2.514e-4 m; Kn_2 = 0.0126, above 0.01 (at the mean pressure it is 0.0082). rho_av = 4.298e-4 kg/m3 at 5.20 Pa; sqrt(RT/M) = 110.0 m/s; x = 152.5; factor 0.8107. Poiseuille term 8.167e-7 kg/s = 2.940 g/h; molecular term 9.115e-8 kg/s = 0.3282 g/h, times 0.8107 = 0.2660 g/h. Sum 3.206 g/h.

Most tempting wrong answer: 2.9 g/h, from the laminar equation (the paper's Eq. (7) gives 2.928, plain Poiseuille flow at the mean density 2.940), chosen because Re is 9 and the Knudsen number at the mean pressure is under 0.01. Others: Poiseuille with a first-order slip factor 1 + 8 Kn, 3.1 g/h; Poiseuille plus free-molecular flow without Knudsen's factor, 3.3 g/h; free-molecular flow alone, 0.33 g/h.
