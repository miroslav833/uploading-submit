The count is of day layers in the endocuticle, eleven of them. The orange exocuticle is pre-ecdysial and is skipped. Day layers are smooth (non-lamellate), night layers striped (lamellate), as in Sviben et al. 2020 for locust tibia.

Read back from the image in three vertical strips: 11 striped and 11 smooth runs each time. The thin smooth run peaks at green 161 to 165, ordinary day layers about 169; the brightest point between lamellae inside any night band is 150 to 152. So the thin line is day cuticle, not a gap between lamellae.

Read the doubled block as one night and you get 10, the likeliest wrong answer. Counting every band gives 22.
