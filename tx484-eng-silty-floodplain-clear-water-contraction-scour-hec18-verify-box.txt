Key: 1.2 m (contraction scour ys over the set-back overbank). In the attached notes it is the KEY line of key.py (ys = 1.1950 m). The deciding point: Eq. 6.4 rests on Shields-type critical shear, which reaches its minimum near 0.2 mm and rises for silts as cohesion acts, so HEC-18, 5th ed. (FHWA-HIF-12-003, 2012), Section 6.4, p. 6.12, takes 0.2 mm as the lower limit of D50 in this equation ('Using a size smaller than 0.2 mm will over-estimate clear-water contraction scour'; reason in Section 6.7, p. 6.15, Figure 6.9). By hand: Dm = 1.25 x 0.2 mm = 0.25 mm; q = 118/84.0 = 1.405 m2/s; y2 = (0.025 x 1.405^2/0.00025^(2/3))^(3/7) = 2.945 m; ys = 2.945 - 1.75 = 1.195 m. The tempting answer is 2.4 m, from the silt's own D50 of 0.060 mm. Check program (standard library): print(round((0.025*(118/84)**2/0.00025**(2/3))**(3/7)-1.75, 4))  prints 1.195.
