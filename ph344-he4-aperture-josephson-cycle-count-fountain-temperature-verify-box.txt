By hand. h f = m4 (dP/rho - s dT). The frequency fell from its heater-off value without passing through zero, so the pressure term stays larger. h/m4 = 9.96930e-8 m^2/s. dP/rho = 0.07962/145.834 = 5.45964e-4 J/kg, heater-off frequency f0 = 5476.45 Hz. dT = (h/m4)(f0 - f)/s = 9.96930e-8 x 459.6/1262 = 3.631e-8 K, i.e. 36 nK. The rounding of the givens keeps 36.20 to 36.42 nK.

The tempting slips: the other branch gives 830 nK; the pair mass 2 m4 gives 230 nK; one cycle fewer gives 39 nK.
