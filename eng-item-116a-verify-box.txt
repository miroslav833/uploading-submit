Key: 1.50 m/s2 (1.496 m/s2) governing transverse acceleration. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY: 1.496 m/s2 at 105 deg (Hs fraction 0.70)" of key.py and "governing incident angle 105 deg: 70 % of 7.50 m = 5.250 m; 0.285 x 5.250 = 1.496 m/s2" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H202 (October 2015) [2.1.5.2] guidance note, page 13: applicable Hs in ULS as % of the design sea state, 100 % at 0° +/- 30°, 80 % at 60°, 60 % at 90°, 80 % at 120°, 100 % from 150° to 180°, linear interpolation between. At 105° the percentage is 70, so 0.70 x 7.50 x 0.285 = 1.496; the next largest products are 1.290 at 120° and 1.260 at 75°. The most tempting wrong answer, 1.28 m/s2, applies one 60 % factor outside the head sector (0.60 x 7.50 x 0.285). Check: print(round(0.70*7.50*0.285,4)) prints 1.4962. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2015-10/OS-H202.pdf, page 13.
