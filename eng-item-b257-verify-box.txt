Key: 1.11 x 10^3 kg (1109.4 kg) total net anode mass. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY total net anode mass M = 1109.4 kg" of key.py and "net anode mass = 1109.4 kg" of independent.py. DNV-RP-F103 (October 2010) 5.2.2 Guidance note, page 8: "For CP calculations, a minimum cut-back length of 0.20 m should be applied" (5.6.4 GN, page 11: r = 0.033 for 12 m joints). A_FJC = 2000 x pi x 0.324 x 0.40 = 814.3 m2; I_cm = 0.070 (24 429 x 0.00145 + 814.3 x 0.0750) = 6.7546 A; M = 6.7546 x 30 x 8760/1600 = 1109.4 kg. The most tempting wrong answer, 934 kg, keeps the actual 150 mm cut-back. Check: import math; c=math.pi*0.324; i=0.07*(c*24000*0.00145+2000*c*0.4*0.075); print(round(i*30*8760/1600,1)) prints 1109.4. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2010-10/RP-F103.pdf, pages 8 and 11.
