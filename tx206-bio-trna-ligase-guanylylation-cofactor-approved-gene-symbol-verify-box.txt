Key: TSLIG1. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and as the last line of section 3. By hand: a small protein conserved in all three domains of life, encoded next to rtcB in prokaryotes, that with DDX1 facilitates formation of the RTCB-guanylate intermediate is archease (Popow et al. 2014; Desai et al. 2014). The HGNC record of the human archease gene, HGNC:24094 (UniProt Q8IWT0, 1p35.1), has the approved symbol TSLIG1, tRNA splicing ligase complex subunit 1, the previous symbol ZBTB8OS, the alias ARCH and the date of the symbol change 2026-06-08. A query of the HGNC web service for the symbol ZBTB8OS finds no record, and a query for it as a previous symbol returns TSLIG1. Most tempting wrong answer: ZBTB8OS, approved until 8 June 2026. Others: ARCH (alias), TSLIG2 (ashwin, formerly C2orf49), TSLIG3B (formerly FAM98B). Check in Python on the copied rows: rows=[('TSLIG1','archease','ZBTB8OS'),('TSLIG2','ashwin','C2orf49'),('RTCB','','C22orf28')]; print([r[0] for r in rows if r[1]=='archease']) prints ['TSLIG1'].
