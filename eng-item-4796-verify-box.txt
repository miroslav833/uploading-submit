Key: 3.5 mm/s (3.497 mm/s) superficial velocity. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY u_f = 3.4967 mm/s" of key.py and "u = 3.4967 mm/s" of independent.py. Kataoka, Yoshida and Yamada, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 6, 172 (1973), Eq. (22), page 173 (also in the abstract): ((1-eps)/eps)^(1/3) k Sc'^(2/3)/(u_f/eps) = 1.85 Re'^(-2/3), with Re' = Dp u_f rho/((1-eps) mu) and u_f the superficial velocity (page 177). By hand: a = 6400 1/m, k = 2.344e-4 m/s; alpha = 0.2105, De' = 0.6994 x 9.31e-9 = 6.511e-9 m2/s; Sh = 21.60 = 1.85 x 1.703 x 5.157 x Re^(1/3), Re = 2.349, u = 3.50 mm/s, Re' = 3.67. The most tempting wrong answer, 9.7 mm/s, uses the handbook form Sh = 1.85 ((1-eps)/eps)^(1/3) Re^(1/3) Sc^(1/3) with the superficial Reynolds number, which is eps^(1/3) below the paper's equation in k. Check: k=7.5e-3*2000/(6400*10); a=1.96/9.31; D=(3.81*a**3-4.73*a**2+2.417*a+0.3646)*9.31e-9; print(round(k**3*0.36**2*6e-4**2/(1.85**3*0.64*D**2)*1e3,3)) prints 3.497.
