Key: 2990 kN (2989.9 kN) characteristic transverse load. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY transverse load = 2989.9 kN (inertia 2789.9 + wind 200.0)" of key.py and "total 2989.9 kN" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H202 (October 2015) [3.2.3.2] guidance note, page 18: on a barge smaller than the North Sea barge (91.4 m x 27.4 m) the limiting wave height can be kept if the Table 3-2 accelerations are multiplied by max{LNSB/L, BNSB/B} = max(91.4/72.0, 27.4/25.0) = 1.2694. Tabulated a_y = 0.37 + 0.017 x 9.50 = 0.5315 g, scaled 0.6747 g; 0.6747 x 421.5 x 9.81 = 2789.9 kN; wind 0.5 x 400 = 200.0 kN; total 2989.9 kN. The most tempting wrong answers are 2610 kN (breadth ratio 1.096) and 2400 kN (no scaling). Check: print(round(421.5*9.81*(0.37+0.017*9.5)*max(91.4/72.0, 27.4/25.0)+200,1)) prints 2989.9. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2015-10/OS-H202.pdf, page 18.
