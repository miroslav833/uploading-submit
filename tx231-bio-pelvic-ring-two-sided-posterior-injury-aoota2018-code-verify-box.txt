Key: 61C2.2(b,k). The key is in the attached notes in the output of the first script (line KEY) and again in the output of the second script (line code).
By hand, from the section Pelvic ring of the 2018 compendium (J Orthop Trauma 32 Suppl 1, S71 to S76, group 61C2 on S75). The type follows the posterior arch: 61A intact, 61B incomplete disruption, 61C complete disruption. Right side: both sacroiliac ligament groups torn and 14 mm of upward shift, a complete disruption, so 61C. Left side: impaction of the sacral ala with the posterior ligaments intact, an incomplete disruption. Group 61C2 is the one with a complete disruption on one side and an incomplete one on the other. Its subgroups follow the complete side: 61C2.1 ilium, 61C2.2 sacroiliac joint, 61C2.3 sacrum. The right disruption runs through the joint, so 61C2.2. Qualifications of 61C2: b bilateral pubic rami fracture, d symphysis disruption, k contralateral posterior lateral compression lesion of the sacrum. Rami broken on both sides give b, the 4 mm symphysis is not disrupted, and the left sacral lesion gives k.
Wrong answers that tempt most. 61C1.2(b) reads the injury as one-sided. 61C2.3(b,k) takes the subgroup from the left sacral fracture. 61C2.2(e,j) has the right subgroup and letters that the page does not give for these findings.
Check in seconds (Python, standard library):
complete_site, other_side, rami, symphysis_open = 'sacroiliac joint', 'compression sacrum', 'bilateral', False
sub = {'ilium': '1', 'sacroiliac joint': '2', 'sacrum': '3'}[complete_site]
q = [{'bilateral': 'b', 'one side': 'a'}[rami]] + ['d'] * symphysis_open + [{'compression sacrum': 'k', 'compression ilium': 'l'}[other_side]]
print('61C2.' + sub + '(' + ','.join(q) + ')')   # 61C2.2(b,k)
