Key: 1.97 m (1.969 m) limiting significant wave height. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY sector +/-35 deg: rate 0.193, Hs = 1.9689 m" of key.py and "sector +/-35 deg; largest Hs = 1.9689 m; first direction exceeded: 35 deg" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H206 (September 2014) [3.2.2.3] guidance note, page 27: with long-crested seas the vessel response is analysed for wave directions at least ±20° outside the vessel heading range, and the heading range is ±15° here, so the sector is ±35°. Largest response inside it: 0.193 m/s per m at 35°. Crane tip may contribute 0.500 - 0.120 = 0.380 m/s, so Hs = 0.380/0.193 = 1.969 m. The most tempting wrong answers are 2.79 m (±15° heading range only, 0.380/0.136) and 2.24 m (±30°, 0.380/0.170). Check: print(round((0.500-0.120)/0.193,3)) prints 1.969. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-09/OS-H206.pdf, page 27.
