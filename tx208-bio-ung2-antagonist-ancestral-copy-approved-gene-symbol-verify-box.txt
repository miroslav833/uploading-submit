Key: RUMY1. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and as the last line of section 3. By hand: the protein that binds UNG2, lowers its level and is needed for class switching and hypermutation is FAM72A (Feng et al. 2021; Rogier et al. 2021). Humans have four FAM72 genes on chromosome 1, copied together with SRGAP2 (Kutzner et al. 2015); the one beside SRGAP2 itself at 1q32.1 is the former FAM72A. Its HGNC record, HGNC:24044 (UniProt Q5TYM5, 1q32.1), has the approved symbol RUMY1, the previous symbol FAM72A and the date of the symbol change 2026-07-06. A query of the HGNC web service for the symbol FAM72A finds no record, and a query for it as a previous symbol returns RUMY1. Most tempting wrong answer: FAM72A, approved until 6 July 2026. The duplicates are RUMY2 (1p11.2, formerly FAM72B), RUMY3 and RUMY4 (1q21.1, formerly FAM72C and FAM72D). Check in Python on the copied rows: rows=[('RUMY1','FAM72A','1q32.1'),('RUMY2','FAM72B','1p11.2'),('RUMY3','FAM72C','1q21.1'),('RUMY4','FAM72D','1q21.1')]; print([r[0] for r in rows if r[2]=='1q32.1']) prints ['RUMY1'].
