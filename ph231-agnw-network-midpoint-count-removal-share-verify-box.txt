By hand. n L^2 = 135 x 900 / 5412.6 = 22.448. Mean |sin| of the angle between two wires for directions within 40.0 deg either side: (2 x 0.69813 - sin 1.39626)/(2 x 0.69813^2) = 0.42210, so 9.4752 crossings per wire. Isotropic threshold 5.637 x 2/pi = 3.5886 crossings per wire. Broken share 1 - 3.5886/9.4752 = 0.6213, i.e. 0.62. Counts 133 to 136 all give 0.62.

The most tempting wrong answer is 0.57, from a density judged from the wires cut by the sides of the rectangle (about 120 midpoints). Ignoring the alignment gives 0.75; |sin| of one wire's angle to the coating direction gives 0.52; the surviving share is 0.38.

A check in Python, standard library only:

import math
t = math.radians(40.0)
m = (2*t - math.sin(2*t))/(2*t*t)
B = 135/(93.0*58.2)*30.0**2*m
print(1 - 5.637*2/math.pi/B)   # 0.6213
