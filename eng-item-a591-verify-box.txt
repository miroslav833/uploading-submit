Key: 1840 kN least minimum breaking load. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY MBL >= 1840.0 kN" of key.py and "gamma_sf = 2.875, least MBL = 1840.0 kN (first product alone: 1684.8 kN)" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H205 (April 2014) [4.1.5.1], page 25: gamma_sf is the greatest of gamma_f gamma_c gamma_r gamma_w gamma_m gamma_tw and 2.3 gamma_r gamma_w gamma_tw. With hand-spliced eyes gamma_r = gamma_s = 1.25 ([4.1.5.5] guidance note, page 26); first product 1.2 x 1.3 x 1.35 x 1.25 = 2.6325, second product 2.3 x 1.25 = 2.875, which governs; MBL = 2.875 x 640 = 1840 kN. The most tempting wrong answer, 1680 kN, stops at the first product. Check: print(round(max(1.2*1.3*1.35, 2.3)*1.25*640,1)) prints 1840.0. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2014-04/OS-H205.pdf, page 25.
