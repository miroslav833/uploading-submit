Image content, as text. Four droplets, each a two-armed spiral of bright bands, viewed along the radial defect.
A: 0.80 mol %, band spacing 2.00 um, bright arms turn counterclockwise going outward from the centre.
B: 2.00 mol %, band spacing 1.25 um, clockwise.
C: 0.50 mol %, band spacing 2.50 um, clockwise.
D: 1.50 mol %, band spacing 1.60 um, counterclockwise.
Scale bar 5 um in panel D. 2.4 to 3.5 bands per droplet radius, so the sense shows at the centre and along every arm.

Key. Pitch = 2 x band spacing: 4.00, 2.50, 5.00, 3.20 um. Twisting power 1/(p x): 0.3125, 0.2000, 0.4000, 0.2083 um^-1 per mol %. Same viewing side in all four, so equal spiral sense means equal sign: A and D against B and C. Fifth mixture: 0.2031 + 0.1354 - 0.2600 - 0.4400 = -0.3615 um^-1, pitch 2.767 um. E_c = (pi^2/p) sqrt(K22/(eps0 d_eps)) = 9.870 x 0.3615 x 0.2376 V = 0.848 V/um.

Most tempting wrong answer: no signs, 0.2031 + 0.2600 + 0.4400 + 0.1354 = 1.0385 um^-1, E_c = 2.44 V/um. D read with the sense of B and C: 0.2031 - 0.8354 = -0.6323, 1.48 V/um. Band spacing taken as the pitch: 1.70 V/um. Other sign patterns: 0.263, 0.372, 0.581, 1.22, 1.80 V/um.
