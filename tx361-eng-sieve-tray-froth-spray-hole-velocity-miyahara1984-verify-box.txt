Key: 10.7 m/s (10.706 m/s). It stands in the attached notes in the section 'Key' (line 'KEY Vh = 10.7059 m/s') and in the section 'Independent route' (line 'Bo = 3.664, Vh = 10.7059 m/s'). Source: Miyahara and Takahashi, J. Chem. Eng. Japan 17(2), 211-214 (1984), https://doi.org/10.1252/jcej.17.211, open access, page 212, Eq. (1): H/D = 0.2 (Fr*)^1.23 (Bo)^0.8 for perforated plates with 1.5 < Bo < 4, 1.5 < Fr* < 10; Bo and Fr* as in the nomenclature on page 214. By hand: Bo = 747 x 9.81 x (3.00e-3)^2/0.0180 = 3.664 (< 4); Bo^0.8 = 2.826; H/D = 9.20/3.00 = 3.067; Fr*^1.23 = 3.067/(0.2 x 2.826) = 5.426; Fr* = 3.955; sqrt(747 x 9.81 x 3.00e-3/3.00) = 2.707 m/s; Vh = 3.955 x 2.707 = 10.71 m/s. Most tempting wrong answers: 16.2 m/s with the single-orifice constant 0.12, 15.2 m/s with the Bo > 4 line, and 5.5 m/s from a momentum balance without the paper.
