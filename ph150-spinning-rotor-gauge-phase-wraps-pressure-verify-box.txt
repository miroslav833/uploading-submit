The figure is a simulated record (620 x 300 px) of the phase lag of the ball behind the reference, modulo one turn, from t = 1500.0 s to 1705.0 s after the switch-off. The time axis has a tick every 50 s (1500 s at column 90, 1700 s at column 583, 2.47 px per second); the phase axis runs from 0 (row 249) to 1 turn (row 21). The record starts at 0.30 turn and ends at 0.68 turn and wraps from 1 back to 0 65 times in between; the lines are 8.1 px apart at the start and 7.3 px at the end. Ten wraps span 81 px (33.0 s) at the start and 73 px (29.7 s) at the end. A horizontal line at 0.40 turn is crossed at t = 1500.2, 1533.2, 1565.6, 1597.4, 1628.6, 1659.2, 1689.2 and 1703.9 s for crossings 1, 11, 21, 31, 41, 51, 61 and 66. The model curve is Phi(t) = Phi(0) + 405.00 t - (405.00/gamma)(1 - exp(-gamma t)) turns with gamma = 4.9159e-7 per second.

Key by hand. Free molecular flow with full accommodation: shear stress 2 p v_t/(pi cbar), torque 16 p omega a^4/(3 cbar), moment of inertia (8 pi/15) rho a^5, so -omega_dot/omega = 10 p/(pi rho a cbar). cbar = sqrt(8 R T/(pi M)) = 473.02 m/s; 10/(pi x 7700 x 0.002250 x 473.02) = 3.8842e-4 per second per pascal. Lag behind a reference at nu0, t from the switch-off: Phi(t) ~ Phi(0) + nu0 gamma t^2/2 - nu0 gamma^2 t^3/6, wrap rate nu0 gamma t. From the wrap time alone: 1/(405.00 x 3.30 x 1517) = 4.93e-7 per second at the start, 1/(405.00 x 2.97 x 1689) = 4.92e-7 per second at the end. From the whole record: lag gained 65 + 0.68 - 0.30 = 65.38 turns; leading order gamma = 2 x 65.38/(405.00 x (1705.0^2 - 1500.0^2)) = 4.914e-7 per second, 4.916e-7 with the cubic term; gas part 4.916e-7 - 0.620e-7 = 4.296e-7 per second; p = 4.296e-7/3.8842e-4 = 1.106e-3 Pa. Answer 1.1e-3 Pa. The two local readings both give 1.11e-3 Pa. Any lag between 62.5 and 67.6 turns (63 to 67 wraps) gives the same two figures, so the result does not rest on an exact tally. The residual rate alone would give 8.2 turns.

The most tempting wrong answer is 1.3e-3 Pa: the residual rate not subtracted, 4.916e-7/3.8842e-4 = 1.27e-3. Other values: wrap rate taken as constant at its value at the start of the record 1.2e-3 Pa; root-mean-square speed in place of the mean speed 1.2e-3 Pa; record treated as beginning at the switch-off 2.0e-2 Pa; diameter in place of the radius 2.2e-3 Pa; factor 1/2 of the phase law left out 4.7e-4 Pa; wrap time judged too long by eye, 3.5 s and 3.2 s at the two ends in place of 3.30 s and 2.97 s (60 to 62 wraps in place of 65) 1.0e-3 Pa, 4 s at the start (54 wraps) 8.9e-4 Pa.

Check in a second (Python 3, standard library):
import math
cbar = math.sqrt(8*8.314462618*296.0/(math.pi*28.01e-3))
K = 10/(math.pi*7.70e3*2.250e-3*cbar)                     # 3.884e-4 per s per Pa
ta, tr, nu0, Phi = 1500.0, 1705.0, 405.00, 65 + 0.68 - 0.30
g = 2*Phi/(nu0*(tr**2 - ta**2))
for _ in range(6): g = 2*(Phi + nu0*g*g*(tr**3 - ta**3)/6)/(nu0*(tr**2 - ta**2))
print(cbar, K, g, (g - 6.20e-8)/K)                         # 473.0 3.884e-4 4.916e-7 1.106e-3
print(g/K)                                                  # 1.27e-3, residual rate not subtracted
print((1/(nu0*3.30*1517) - 6.20e-8)/K, (1/(nu0*2.97*1689) - 6.20e-8)/K)   # 1.11e-3 1.11e-3, wrap time alone
