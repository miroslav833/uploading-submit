Key: 2.8 m (2.7715 m). The notes print it in 'Key, wrong forms and half-last-digit table' (line 'KEY') and in 'Independent route' (RK4 march of the bulk temperature, 2.77147 m).

By hand: rho_m = 0.05 x 2500 + 0.95 x 992.2 = 1067.59 kg/m3; solids mass fraction 0.11709, C_m = 3788.05 J/kg K; W = 0.40247 kg/s, Q = W C_m (60.0 - 20.0) = 60983 W. Harada et al. (1985), Eq. (3), p. 35, and nomenclature, p. 37: Nu Pr^-1/3 (mu_f/mu_w)^-0.14 = 1.61e-2 Re^0.88 with Re = D U rho_m/mu_f = 39238 and Pr = C_m mu_f/k_f = 3.9201; Nu = 0.0161 x 11027 x 1.5768 x 1.08907 = 304.86, h = 9618 W/m2K. dT_lm = 40/ln 3 = 36.410 K; L = 60983/(9618 pi 0.02 x 36.410) = 2.771 m.

Most tempting wrong answer, 3.9 m: the single-phase Sieder-Tate equation 0.027 Re^0.8 Pr^1/3 (mu/mu_w)^0.14 on the same groups, a factor 1.39 below Eq. (3) at this Re. Its 0.023 variant (Nu = 186.9, h = 5.90e3 W/m2K) gives 4.5 m. The paper's Fig. 6 shows its suspension data above the Sieder-Tate line.
