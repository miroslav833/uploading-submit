Key by hand. Plane-wave modulation instability in a 1D Kerr medium: gain g = (kappa/2k) sqrt(4 k gamma - kappa^2), k = 2 pi n0/lambda, gamma = k n2 I/n0, peak at kappa^2 = 2 k gamma = 8 pi^2 n0 n2 I/lambda^2. So I = lambda^2/(2 n0 n2 Lambda^2) = (1.5500e-6)^2/(2 x 2.437 x 3.453e-18 x (35.273e-6)^2) = 1.1474e14 W/m^2 = 11.5 GW/cm^2.

Other values. Factor 2 dropped in the peak condition: 22.9. Vacuum wavenumber for k: 28.0. Edge of the gain band (kappa^2 = 4 k gamma): 5.74. 142 or 144 periods: 11.3 or 11.6.
