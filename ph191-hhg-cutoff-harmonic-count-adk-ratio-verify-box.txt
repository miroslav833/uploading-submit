That is 46 lines and 45 spacings (10 lines in columns 182 to 230, 10 in 230 to 290, 11 in 290 to 365, 9 in 365 to 450, 6 in 450 to 501); the spacing falls from 10.7 px at H129 to under 5 px at the cutoff.

Key by hand. Odd harmonics only, so q_c = 129 + 2 x 45 = 219. Photon energy 1239.842/1810 = 0.68500 eV; 219 x 0.68500 = 150.014 eV. U_p = (150.014 - 21.5645)/3.17 = 40.520 eV = 1.48910 hartree. U_p = F^2/(4 w^2) with w = 0.025173 hartree gives F = 0.06144 a.u. kappa = 1.25895, 2/kappa = 1.58862. Prefactor 2.10^2 x 1.5 x kappa^(1 - 1.58862) = 5.7765; (2 kappa^3/F)^0.58862 = 11.6669; exp(-21.6525). Rate 2.6610e-8 a.u. = 1.1001e-6 per fs. Answer 1.1e-6 fs^-1.

Most tempting wrong answer: a count one spacing short (44 spacings, cutoff 217) gives 9.8e-7 fs^-1; one spacing long gives 1.2e-6 fs^-1. Taking every line as one order (cutoff 174) gives 4.9e-8 fs^-1.

Check program (Python 3, standard library; prints the rate for 42 to 48 spacings):
import math
HA = 27.211386; w = 1239.841984 / 1810 / HA; ip = 21.5645 / HA; k = math.sqrt(2 * ip)
def rate(n):                     # n = line spacings from H129 to the cutoff line
    q = 129 + 2 * n; up = (q * w - ip) / 3.17; F = 2 * w * math.sqrt(up)
    return 2.10 ** 2 * 1.5 * k ** (1 - 2 / k) * (2 * k ** 3 / F) ** (2 / k - 1) * math.exp(-2 * k ** 3 / (3 * F)) / 0.024188843
print(" ".join(f"{n}:{rate(n):.2g}" for n in range(42, 49)))
