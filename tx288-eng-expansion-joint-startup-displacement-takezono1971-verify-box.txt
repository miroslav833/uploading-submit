Key: 6.4 mm. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and in the last line of section 3. By hand, with Takezono, Bull. JSME 14 (76), 1021 (1971), Eq. (3): N = 1.00e7/(sigma_em - 23)^2, sigma_em in kgf/mm2. Stress per mm 82.5 MPa = 8.413 kgf/mm2. Vibration cycles: 8.413 x 3.50 = 29.444 kgf/mm2, N = 1e7/6.444^2 = 2.408e5, damage 73 000/2.408e5 = 0.303. Start-ups: N = 7300/0.697 = 1.0475e4, sigma - 23 = sqrt(1e7/1.0475e4) = 30.90, sigma = 53.90 kgf/mm2 = 528.5 MPa, delta = 528.5/82.5 = 6.41 mm. The most tempting wrong answer uses the Langer line of the same steel, 2.24e7/(sigma - 23)^2, which the paper shows lies above the joint data: it gives 8.9 mm. Leaving the vibration cycles out gives 7.1 mm. Check program (Python): k=82.5/9.80665; N=lambda s:1e7/(s-23)**2; Ns=7300/(1-73000/N(k*3.5)); print(round((23+(1e7/Ns)**0.5)/k,1)) prints 6.4.
