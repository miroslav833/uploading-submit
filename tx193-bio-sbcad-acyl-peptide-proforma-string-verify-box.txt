Key: GTC[UNIMOD:4]VDNWAIK[UNIMOD:2109]YEFMQDLKPGTEAR. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again in section 3, built by a separate digest and record choice. By hand: ACADSB loss blocks the isoleucine pathway at (S)-2-methylbutanoyl-CoA (Andresen et al., AJHG 67, 1095, 2000), which acylates lysine without an enzyme; 2-methylbutyryl adds C5H8O, 84.0575 Da. Trypsin cuts after R6, not after acylated K16, not at K24-P25, then after R30, so the peptide is residues 7 to 30, GTCVDNWAIKYEFMQDLKPGTEAR. Carbamidomethyl cysteine is Unimod 4; 2-methylbutyryl lysine is Unimod 2109 (isobars: isovaleryl 2108, pentanoyl 2120). Most tempting wrong answer: the same string with UNIMOD:2108 (isovaleryl, the leucine-pathway isomer of isovaleric acidaemia). Cutting after the acylated lysine gives GTC[UNIMOD:4]VDNWAIK[UNIMOD:2109]. Check in Python: import re; s='MALSERGTCVDNWAIkYEFMQDLKPGTEARSWVHDK'; p=[x for x in re.split(r'(?<=[KR])(?!P)',s) if 'k' in x][0]; print(p.upper().replace('C','C[UNIMOD:4]').replace('IKY','IK[UNIMOD:2109]Y')) prints the key.
