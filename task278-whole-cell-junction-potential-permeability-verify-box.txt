The figure crosses zero at -30.0 mV. That voltage still carries the junction between pipette solution and Ringer, because the offset was nulled in Ringer before the seal. Henderson equation, mobilities from Barry's table (gluconate 0.33, Ng and Barry 1995): +16.3 mV. True reversal -46.3 mV.

Pipette: K+ 148.9, gluconate 140, Cl- 5, HEPES- 3.9 mM. Ringer: Na+ 144.4, K+ 5, Ca2+ 2, Mg2+ 1, Cl- 151, HEPES- 4.4 mM. NMDG-Cl: NMDG+ 150, Cl- 145.6, HEPES- 4.4 mM. 22 C, RT/F 25.43 mV.

P_NMDG/P_K = (148.9/150) exp(-46.3/25.43) = 0.16.
