Key by hand. lambda_up = 326.0 mm / 117 = 2.7863 mm, lambda_up f = 278.63 m/s. c0 = sqrt(1.4 x 287.05 x 294.15) = 343.82 m/s. Upstream of a source fixed in the stream lambda_up = (c - U)/f, with c at the static temperature T = T0/(1 + 0.2 M^2): (1 - M)/sqrt(1 + 0.2 M^2) = 0.81041, M = 0.18677, T = 292.11 K, c = 342.62 m/s, U = 63.99 m/s, so 64 m/s.
Most tempting wrong answers: a miscount (116 give 61.7 m/s, 118 give 66.3 m/s, 119 give 68.5 m/s, 120 give 70.7 m/s; the middle spacing gives about 113 and 54 m/s, the end-spacing rule about 123 and 77 m/s) and the settling-chamber sound speed (65.2 m/s).

import math
c0=math.sqrt(1.4*287.05*294.15); t=326.0e-3/117*100e3/c0; M=0.2
for i in range(50): M=1-t*math.sqrt(1+0.2*M*M)
