Key: 110 kN (110.8 kN) least continuous thrust per thruster. In the attached notes it is the line "KEY least thrust per thruster = 110.8 kN" of key.py and "first thrust per thruster that meets both = 110.84 kN" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H203 (February 2012) Sec.3 B202, page 15: for coastal and narrow waters the propulsion should both maintain "a minimum speed of 2 knots in the environmental design condition" and "zero speed in the environmental design condition with failure in any one single part of the propulsion system". 2 kn = 1.0289 m/s: 160 x 1.0289^2 + 1.65 x 19.529^2 + 22 x 4 = 886.7 kN / 8 = 110.8 kN; zero speed 652.7 kN / 7 = 93.2 kN. The most tempting wrong answer, 130 kN, puts the failure on the speed case (886.7/7 = 126.7). Check: v=2*1852/3600; print(round((160*v*v+1.65*(18.5+v)**2+88)/8,1)) prints 110.8. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-02/OS-H203.pdf, page 15.
