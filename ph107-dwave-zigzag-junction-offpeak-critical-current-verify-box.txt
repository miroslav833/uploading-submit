Key by hand. The critical current density changes sign from each facet to the next (d-wave lobes along a and b have opposite signs). With u the distance along the line in units of l and sigma(u) = +1 or -1 the sign, I_c/I_0 is the modulus of the integral of sigma(u) exp(i theta u) over the line. In unit intervals j = 0 ... 163 the sign is (-1)^j up to j = 65 and -(-1)^j from j = 66 on, because the long facet covers the intervals 65 and 66 with one sign. With alpha = theta + pi, A = 66 and M = 164 the sum over the intervals is (1 - 2 exp(i A alpha) + exp(i M alpha))/(1 - exp(i alpha)), and each interval carries the factor (exp(i theta) - 1)/(i theta), of modulus sin(theta/2)/(theta/2). For theta = 7 pi/8: alpha = -pi/8 (mod 2 pi), A alpha = -pi/4 (mod 2 pi), M alpha = -pi/2 (mod 2 pi). The numerator is 1 - 2 exp(-i pi/4) + exp(-i pi/2) = (1 - sqrt 2) + i (sqrt 2 - 1), of modulus 2 - sqrt 2 = 0.5858. The denominator is 2 sin(pi/16) = 0.3902. The facet factor is sin(7 pi/16)/(7 pi/16) = 0.7136. I_c/I_0 = 0.7136 x 0.5858/0.3902 = 1.071, so 1.07.

The most tempting wrong answer is 2.03. The long facet is then counted as an ordinary one and the line is taken as a regular zigzag of 163 facets: 0.7136 x |sin(163 pi/16)|/sin(pi/16) = 0.7136 x 0.5556/0.1951 = 2.03. Other values: a regular zigzag of length 164 l gives 2.59, no sign change between the two kinds of facet gives 0.514, a regular zigzag of eight facets gives 3.66, point sources without the facet factor give 1.50, the reversal put one unit too early or too late gives 1.61, the long facet left out of the sum gives 0.793. A miscount by one facet on either side gives 0.90 or 1.71.

Check in a few seconds (Python 3, standard library). The first number is the key, the second the most tempting wrong answer:
import cmath, math
th = 7*math.pi/8
def ic(lengths):
    z, u = 0, 0
    for k, l in enumerate(lengths):
        z += (-1)**k*(cmath.exp(1j*th*(u + l)) - cmath.exp(1j*th*u))/(1j*th)
        u += l
    return abs(z)
print(ic([1]*65 + [2] + [1]*97))     # 1.0713
print(ic([1]*163))                   # 2.0318
