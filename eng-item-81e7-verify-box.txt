Key: 3450 m3/h intact ballast capacity. In the attached notes it is the line "class 2: intact requirement 3450 m3/h, with one pump system failed 2760 m3/h" of key.py and "governing intact requirement: 3450 m3/h" of independent.py. DNV-OS-H201 (April 2012) Table 3-1, page 18: significant tide range, not tide restricted, weather restricted gives loadout Class 2. Table 2-2, page 13, Class 2: at least 130 % capacity with the intact system in normal operation and at least 150 % in tide compensation (load transfer unexpectedly stopped). 1.30 x 2400 = 3120 and 1.50 x 2300 = 3450; the greater governs. The most tempting wrong answers are 3120 m3/h (normal operation only) and 2880 m3/h (a 20 % margin). Check: print(max(130*2400//100, 150*2300//100)) prints 3450. Source: https://rules.dnv.com/docs/pdf/DNVPM/codes/docs/2012-04/OS-H201.pdf, pages 13 and 18.
