Key: GO:0160191. It stands in section 2 of the attached notes (line KEY) and again at the end of section 3. By hand: campesterol, campestanol and 6-oxocampestanol do not rescue, while 6-deoxocathasterone, cathasterone and all later compounds do. 6-Deoxocathasterone is campestanol hydroxylated at C-22 and cathasterone is 6-oxocampestanol hydroxylated at C-22, so the line lacks the steroid C-22 hydroxylase that gives the 22S alcohol (DWF4, CYP90B1; Choe et al. 1998, Fujita et al. 2006). In go-basic.obo with data-version releases/2026-07-26 the only valid term cross-referenced to EC 1.14.14.178 is steroid 22S-hydroxylase activity, GO:0160191, and the obsolete terms campestanol hydroxylase activity (GO:0102759) and 6-oxocampestanol hydroxylase activity (GO:0102729) both carry replaced_by GO:0160191. Most tempting wrong answer: GO:0010012, steroid 22-alpha hydroxylase activity, the term for this activity until it was made obsolete in November 2024 with replaced_by GO:0160191. A reading of the table that puts the block one step later gives GO:0102097, the C-23 hydroxylase, which fails because cathasterone and 6-deoxocathasterone rescue. GO:0102679 is a valid term for C-22 hydroxylation of (5alpha)-campestan-3-one alone and covers neither blocked conversion.
